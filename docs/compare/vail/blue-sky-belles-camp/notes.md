# Blue Sky Basin, Belle's Camp (Steep & Deep / Cloud Nine sign)

Real: Google Street View trekker, Mar 2012 (calibrated; links in spot.json). The real screenshots were shared in chat and are not saved locally. Sunny. Game: `eye-<view>.jpg`, eye camera at (1586, -4008), 1919x910, 2026-11-06. Ground at 3,490 m.

Position and compass check: the lodge sits at about 207 degrees true in the photo; the nearest building in the pack is 75 m away at 212.

## What matches
- **Terrain:** the flat top around the lodge and the track running east.
- **Lodge position** (south, west).

## What doesn't
1. FIXED by BUG-025 (eye shots re-taken 2026-11-06 after the rebuild: the 310 trees within 100 m are now 6.7 to 23.5 m, p10 to p90, and 6 are krummholz; the colour is still FEAT-070). Before: **The tall spruce-fir stand is drawn as 4.5 m black pyramids (north, east, west).** Real: a closed stand of tall spruce and fir 15 to 30 m from the camera on every side, closing off the view. The pack has 311 trees within 100 m, but **every one is krummholz capped at 4.5 m**: the pipeline turns every tree above 3,350 m into krummholz. 171,089 trees in the Vail pack sit in that 3,350 to 3,550 m band. -> **BUG-025** (#113)
2. **The cat track is a grey ribbon (east, south):** the real track toward the lodge is groomed snow. -> **BUG-026** (#114)
3. **The lodge:** a timber barn-roofed building in reality; the game has a low grey box and a red-roofed lift terminal. -> FEAT-071, DATA-009
4. **Lift line (south, west):** the game shows a lift line through an open gap; in the photo the trees hide it. This follows from item 1.
