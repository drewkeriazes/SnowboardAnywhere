# Mongolia Bowl, open slope above Two Elk Connector

Real: Street View, Feb 2014, contributor Krishna Vinod (links in spot.json). Snowing, low cloud. The real screenshots were shared in chat and are not saved locally; reopen the links in spot.json. Game: `eye-<view>.png`, eye camera at (3790, -238), 1919x910, 2026-11-06.

**Compass about 76 degrees off**, the same as this contributor's In the Wuides panorama from the same month (Yonder was -88). Found from the fall line: downhill is about 270 by the panorama's compass and about 195 grid in the DEM. All four views line up with the correction; view ids are true directions.

## What matches
- **Terrain:** the long open slope rising across the north-west and north-east views, the tree line stepping down the slope (north-east), and the forested valley below (south-west, south-east).
- **Valley forest:** present and dark, roughly the right extent.

## What doesn't
1. **Trees on the open slope:** the real slope has spruce clumps and lone trees scattered across it within 30 to 60 m, and a line of them along the slope (north-west, north-east). The pack has 0 trees within 30 m and 3 within 100 m. -> BUG-023
2. **Weather:** heavy cloud and falling snow in the photo; clear in the game. A snowstorm setting would make this pair a closer match.
