# Blue Sky Basin, near In the Wuides

Real: Street View, Feb 2014, contributor Krishna Vinod (links in spot.json; `real-*.png` local only). Clear winter light. Game: `eye-<view>.jpg`, eye camera at (1041, -3367), 1919x910, 2026-11-06.

**This panorama's compass is about 76 degrees off** (same contributor as Yonder, -88). Found from the fall line: downhill is about 335 degrees by the panorama's compass, and the DEM's fall line here runs about 260 grid. Confirmed in all four views; view ids are true directions.

## What matches
- **Terrain:** the steep slope rising across the view (north, south), looking up to the skyline (east), and the forested valley below with the far hills (west, north).
- **Valley forest (south, west):** the dark forest band below the slope is about right here, better than at most spots.

## What doesn't
1. **Red rock outcrops missing (north):** the real slope has reddish-brown sandstone outcrops poking through the snow just below the ridge. The game has none here. The colour matches the FEAT-069 finding (Vail's ski-area rock is red, not grey); here the game is missing them rather than overdoing them. -> FEAT-069
2. **Lone trees on the open slope (north, south, west):** the real slope has a scatter of short, bushy, snow-dusted conifers within a few tens of metres. The pack has 1 tree within 15 m and 2 within 50 m. -> BUG-023
3. **Aspen bands in the valley (west):** bare aspen shows as grey-brown bands between the dark conifers; the game's valley is all conifer. -> FEAT-070 / aspen classification
4. **Lift line on the skyline (east, south):** the game shows a lift line along the ridge above; none is visible in the photo. It may sit just beyond the real skyline. Not conclusive.
5. **Snow:** chopped, wind-textured powder; the game is smooth. -> FEAT-059 / FEAT-047
