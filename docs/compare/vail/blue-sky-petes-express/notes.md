# Blue Sky Basin, near Pete's Express (Big Rock Park)

Real: Google Street View trekker, Mar 2012 (calibrated; links in spot.json). The real screenshots were shared in chat and are not saved locally. Sunny spring snow. Game: `eye-<view>.png`, eye camera at (1749, -2532), 1919x910, 2026-11-06. 11 m from Big Rock Park - West.

## What matches
- **Terrain:** the run dropping into the gully between forested slopes (south-east), and the slope rising to the north with the distant snowy hill.
- **Forest edges:** the best tree match of all the spots. The pack has 147 trees within 100 m, and the forest walls sit where the photo has them.

## What doesn't
1. **Trees read as black cones.** Real spruce and lodgepole are lit, textured and snow-dusted; the game's are dark and smooth. -> FEAT-070
2. **Aspen as pale cones (north):** the real aspen stand on the right is bare white trunks with fine twig crowns. The game draws pale cones. -> FEAT-070
3. **The big boulder:** a large grey boulder sits in the middle of the run (south-east, the reason for the run's name). The game places rocks off to the left instead. -> FEAT-069
4. **Snow:** spring snow; the game is fresh corduroy. Season.
