# China Bowl, Poppyfields / Dragon's Teeth

Real: Street View, Mar 2014, contributor Josh Jett, listed as "Vail Ski Resort" (links in spot.json; `real-*.png` local only). Clear spring sky. Game: `eye-<view>.jpg`, eye camera at (1583, -30), 1919x910, 2026-11-06. This is the panorama behind the FEAT-069 rock findings.

**This panorama's compass is about 105 degrees off**, the biggest error so far; the pin position is right. Found from the far peaks: by the panorama's compass the snowy range spans 314 to 349 degrees, but from here the high peaks (Holy Cross and the Sawatch) lie at about 210 to 245 degrees. Confirmed in all four views. View ids are true directions. The earlier FEAT-069 "matched" framing at (2561, -537) was for a different photo; that spot does not match these views.

## What matches
- **Terrain:** the saddle between two rounded hills (north), the slope rising to the ridge (west), the forested slope dropping into the valley with the peaks beyond (south), and the forested skyline (east). Close.
- **Lift line** on the skyline (north, east).

## What doesn't
1. **Rock that isn't there (west, north):** the game draws grey rock bands along the ridge and the saddle rim; the real ridge and saddle are clean snow. Confirms FEAT-069 (too much winter rock, and grey instead of Vail's red sandstone).
2. **Trees on the saddle and ridge (north, west):** the real saddle has a small group of lone spruce, and the ridge has several clumps; the game has a few small cones. -> BUG-023
3. **Forested skyline (east) and valley forest (south):** real is a dense dark band; the game's is thinner, and the far valley is pale. -> BUG-023
4. **Snow:** spring snow, tracked and wind-textured; the game is fresh corduroy. Season.
