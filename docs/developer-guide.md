## Features in detail

- Terrain: quadtree LOD over the 262 km far ring, 1 m near the rider, snow / rock / scree / groomed material; forested ground darkens from the tree impostor ring outward so the far forest reads dark.
- Riding: carving on a real edge angle (up to 72 deg on firm snow, 45 in powder) with the turn radius from the board's sidecut, washing into a skid past the grip; edge to edge takes about 0.3 s at speed; the rider leans into the hill up to 60 deg with deep toeside and heelside carve poses. Hard carve (FEAT-148, the squat key held while steering): `Rider.hardCarve` eases in, the edge cap goes to `HARD_PSI` (80 deg) and the edge rolls `HARD_ROLL` times faster (slowing with the square of the speed past `HARD_ROLL_V`), the lean may reach `HARD_PHI` and the snow holds up to `HARD_G` (2.6 g) times its grip; the press asks for the turn of the target edge, driven in over `HARD_DRIVE_T` once the edge is on the steer's side, while the edge holds only what its real angle balances, so a lagging edge (pressed from flat at speed, on ice) skids (`carveShort`, folded into `skid` for the drag, spray and sound) and short of more than `WASH_SHARE` (35%) of the turn asked, at speed, fills `washout` (a share, so softer snow that asks less washes out alike; the snow's grip scales what the edge holds; only deep powder, its share times depth over `HARD_DEEP` 30 cm, lowers the hard carve's edge cap and stops the washout); full, the rider crashes to the inside of the turn (`crashSide`, the ragdoll topples that way) and `onWashout` toasts it. `HARD_DRAG` adds a drag from the lateral acceleration. The view lays the body over to `LEAN_HARD` (73 deg) in the deep carve stance (BUG-066: the squat pose fades out while `hardCarve` is in, and `RiderView.deckDeficit` lifts the hips until the shins, knees and thighs clear the deck in the board's own frame, `DECK_CLEAR`) and drags the inside hand on the snow; the spray, the track depth and the carve hiss rise with it; `?debug` shows hard, short and wash. Also tuck, brake (the board swings across into a hockey stop, scrapes a wide track and holds on its edge once stopped; steering while braking digs the edge in for a harder stop or lets it off into a skidded turn or a side-slip), charged jumps with ollie and nollie off the board's spring (hold W / S with Space), nose and tail presses (Q / Z) balanced on a meter (FEAT-140: `Rider.pressStep`, the needle `pressBalance` drifts with the hold time, speed and bumps, runs away off centre, and W / S or the stick shift the weight back (over 0.15 s, and always strong enough to win, so there is no time limit); off the loaded end a slam that topples the ragdoll over that end (`Rider.crashFall`) or a stall, off the other end the board drops flat; a press key held through a trick lands into the press (`pressFrom`); `onPress` reports the time and distance, `ui/PressMeter.ts` draws the meter over the head, tunables `PB_*` and `PRESS_RANGE` (how far the balance runs before it is lost; the body leans in proportion) at the top of `Rider.ts`, Settings > Game > Press balance Easy / Normal / Hard / Off), squat (Left Ctrl), skating on the flat (X), grabs, air spins, front flips and backflips (Up / Down in the air, trick names toast on landing: flips, and spins as "Frontside 180" / "Backside 360"), landings; collisions with trees, rocks and building walls. The snowboard bends when loaded. Snowboard or skis, outfit colours, articulated rider with blended poses. The body leads and the board follows (BUG-056, visual only): the head and shoulders pose about 0.2 s ahead of the physics (the steer's target edge and the heading the turn rate is reaching), the hips and the body's inclination about 0.12 s ahead, each lead signal followed through a critically damped 0.09 s body response (`BODY_RESPONSE`) so the body moves at a human speed instead of snapping to its prediction, and the board as drawn trails the physics yaw and edge by about 0.08 s (critically damped, never rolled past the physics edge, snapped on landings and crashes), so an edge change reads look, shoulders, hips, knees, board. Tunables `LEAD_UPPER`, `LEAD_HIPS`, `BOARD_LAG` at the top of `RiderView.ts`, live on `riderView.leadUpper` / `leadHips` / `boardLag` and as sliders under `?debug`.
- Riding switch and stance (FEAT-139): `rider.heading` is always the steering axis along the direction of travel, and every physics value (edge, lateral g, lean, flip, brake side) is in its frame, so + edge is the heading's right-hand edge. Going backwards along it faster than `FAKIE_SPEED` (2.5 m/s), or landing with the board backwards, `turnRound()` adds PI to the heading and board yaw, negates the edge, lean and lateral g, and toggles `rider.switch`. `rider.goofy` comes from Settings > Rider > Stance. `rider.toeSign` is -1 when switch and goofy differ: `RiderView` then draws the board and body at the yaw + PI and negates every sideways and fore-aft value into the rig's frame (`fl`), so turning round never moves the rider on screen and the toe and heel poses stay the rider's own. A goofy rig (`buildRig(..., goofy)`) has the deck's nose at +Z and the +15 / -6 bindings swapped ends, so drawn turned round it is a true goofy stance. Seated and on foot are always drawn along the heading; unstrapping and strapping in turn the heading round when needed so nothing pops. Switch is 4 % less grip (`SWITCH_GRIP`), shows SWITCH on the HUD, and prefixes trick names ("Switch backside 180"). Replays and ghosts carry flag bits 8 (switch) and 16 (goofy). Skis never ride switch.
- Snow conditions (FEAT-053, `sim/SnowConditions.ts`): `snowAt()` turns the surface mask, the ground normal (slope and aspect), the elevation, the canopy and the day (`rider.snowWorld`: hour, storm, new snow cover, the Snow conditions setting, the pack's elevation range, set by `main.ts` each frame) into weights for groomed / powder / chop / ice / slush / rock that sum to 1, plus the physics they blend (`grip`, `drag0`, `drag1`, `turn`, `jolt`, `skidScrub`, `depth`). Powder keeps in the shade of north aspects, high up, under the trees and after a storm (a storm or a powder day makes the groomers ride soft too); open off-piste is tracked out into chop as the day goes on; wind scours high exposed ground to ice and spring mornings refreeze the groomers; the afternoon sun makes slush low down on the sunny faces (most of all in spring). In `Rider.ts` the state scales the grip (ice about half), the sidecut's arc and the lean's turn force (powder 0.7: wider, slower turns), the snow drag (powder speed-scaled until the board planes at `PLANE_SPEED`, the nose sinking and digging in below `SINK_SPEED`, slush heavy), how much a skid scrubs speed (ice little), and chop adds seeded random jolts to the heading and a sideways kick (`CHOP_*`, `rider.chop` for the camera). `bootSink` reads the depth on foot. The spray (`RiderView.updateSpray`: a plume off the edge in powder, almost none on ice), the camera rumble, the track depth and the sound (`Audio.update`: ice on the hardpack layer, slush a wet hush, chop a crackle; footsteps too) follow the weights. The HUD names the dominant kind while riding and shows the kind and the boot sink on foot; `?debug` adds the weights.
- Crashes: a Verlet ragdoll takes over the skeleton, tumbles against the snow, trunks, rocks and walls, follows the sim's crash slide and blends back to the animated pose as the rider gets up.
- On foot: B unstraps when nearly stopped (or straps back in standing still); walk with the board carried (on the shoulder when sprinting), sprint on Shift (stamina), step up and climb onto platforms, bootprints in the snow.
- Snow: 3D tracks pressed into the snow behind the rider (carves narrow to a knife-cut line); tracks and bootprints fill in faster while it snows, and new snow buries terrain rock from its edges in.
- Deformable snow and powder spray (FEAT-044, design in `docs/research/deformable-snow-and-spray.md`): a hybrid. The sharp trench is the ribbon (`Tracks.ts`): in soft snow its floor is 0.7 of the loose snow depth (8 to 25 cm in powder, the 5 cm groove on a groomer) and the knife-cut collapse is held back, so a powder carve leaves a trench the board's width, never wider. `world/Deform.ts` renders the track lanes and the boot prints top-down every frame into a 512 px render target over a 64 m window that follows the rider (leading along the travel direction), with MAX blending (R depression, G pushed-up snow, B packed); the lanes and prints are the persistence, so the texture holds no state. Shaders read it only through its mipmap at about 1 m per texel (`deformSag`), so the GPU pre-blurs the stamp to the terrain's 1 m vertex spacing and it cannot alias: the terrain (`TerrainMaterial.ts`), the ribbon and the boot prints all sink by that soft sag at their vertices, and the terrain tilts its normal by the sag's slope. A first build displaced the terrain from the full-resolution texture on a 25 cm split; it beaded along diagonal tracks and cost about 120 draw calls, so it was dropped. `world/Spray.ts` replaces the CPU spray: a ring of 8,192 GPU particles moved analytically in the vertex shader from spawn records (wisps on groomers and ice, clouds that hang, grow and drift on the wind in powder and slush, a low sheet in a hockey stop, a burst on landing), emission from speed x sin(edge angle) x loose depth, the skid and the scrape. Settings > Graphics > Snow dents turns the sag off; the Low preset and `?nodeform` turn it off too; auto quality halves the texture at level 3 and turns it off at level 4, and scales the spray count with the snowfall share.
- World: instanced forests (species from LANDFIRE, impostors at distance), rocks with snow caps, extruded buildings, roads and catwalks draped on the terrain, bridges over gaps the bare-earth lidar removes, piste overlay, peak and town labels on the horizon (hidden behind nearer ridges). Landmarks (FEAT-132) are hand-placed signs OSM does not carry, such as Vail's Legendary Back Bowls gateway arch; its name toasts as you ride under it, and Vail's default start is just above it.
- Lifts: towers, cables and moving chairs from OSM. E at a bottom terminal boards (prompt card, floating markers and a loading zone show where); while riding, E skips to the top and holding W fast-forwards. The rider sits on a real chair. Chairlift bases have lift-line mazes (2 to 4 lanes by capacity, wait and load lines, funnel fences, a name board) whose ropes are colliders; with a maze built, boarding works only inside the corral. Settings > Game turns the mazes off.
- Time trials (Settings > Game): start and finish gates on real runs, splits, a ghost of your best run, boundary walls with a 5 s grace, an arrow to the finish. T jumps to the nearest start.
- Loading screen (BUG-063, `ui/Loading.ts`, `warmUp` in `main.ts`): after the menu, a DOM overlay paints first, then the session builds behind it. Warm-up streams terrain, trees and rocks around the start with a 12 ms per-frame build budget and no rendering, until no tile is downloading and no chunk or tree cell is waiting (20 s cap), then `renderer.compileAsync` compiles the shaders, and the overlay stays up for the first 3 real frames. While riding, chunk builds stop after 4 ms a frame (`Terrain.budgetMs`, max 10) and tree cell builds after 3 ms (`Vegetation.budgetMs`); built parent chunks cover any gaps. Headless runs skip the screen and keep the fixed counts, so scripted runs stay deterministic.
- Outrun (Esc > Outrun, FEAT-097 to FEAT-101; design in `docs/research/outrun-mode.md`): a snow monster chases you down the mountain after a head start. It follows your breadcrumbs by arc length (no pathfinding, any pack), charges straight with line of sight inside 60 m, rubber-bands on your recent speed so crashes and stops feed it, and rides the cable tower to tower when you take a lift (no lift skip while it runs). Difficulties Chill / Normal / SkiFree, escape by reaching the base (lowest building cluster near a lift bottom) or Endless (a second one at 3 min). F burns adrenaline (close calls, clean landings, fast carving) for a 4 s boost, hold G to look back. Spatial sound (HRTF gallop, roars, breathing, heartbeat), music ducks, muffles and cuts as it closes in with war drums from the jukebox. Caught: slow-mo grab, rip in half (snow and confetti, no gore), unhinged jaw, two bites, the board spat out, a burp, then the EATEN card with best scores; Gentle swallows you whole. `node tools/outruncheck.mjs vail shots-outrun --gpu` runs the whole flow headless.
- Replay and rewind (FEAT-043): the session is recorded. K opens the replay viewer (play, scrub, speed, follow / trackside / free camera, FOV, time of day, hide HUD, P photo); holding Backspace rewinds along the recording and letting go carries on from the last good ground (it cancels a time trial).
- Sky and weather: real sun position for the resort's lon/lat and date (resort default, real time, or a fixed hour and month); volumetric clouds from bluebird to broken, plus thin high cloud (streaky cirrus and faint contrails); a snowstorm option.
- Cameras: chase (behind and above, looking over the rider's head at the snow ahead), close (tight and low), far (angled broadcast view looking down at the rider), cinematic (low, off to one side, long lens), first person, helmet cam (wide and level, on top of the helmet) and free fly (backtick). Hold Alt or the right mouse button to look around, hold G to look behind in any camera (FEAT-124). Speed feel: FOV widens with speed, the camera rumbles with the snow under the board (none on corduroy, more off-piste) and shakes on landings, and speed lines streak past; each has a switch in Settings. First person sees through goggles (dark frame, lens tint, snow drift, frost in powder, darken and shake on hard landings) with your legs, board and arms in view.
- Run stats (FEAT-055): each run tracks time, top speed, distance, vertical, longest air, biggest drop, max lateral g, the named runs ridden (nearest mapped run line within 25 m) and the line, sampled every 2 s. A run starts at 3 m/s on the board and ends on boarding a lift, a 4 s stop or a restart; under 60 m of vertical it is dropped. The after-run card shows the summary; the HUD shows the live run. Day totals (vertical, runs, lifts) and per-run personal bests (fastest time, top speed, keyed by the first named run) persist in localStorage under `session-day` and `run-pbs`. Paused during Outrun; no card in headless runs.
- Maps: minimap with nearby lift names, and a full map (M) with zoom, pan and click to drop in anywhere; landmarks show as gold diamonds.
- Sound: layered surface-aware board sound (groomed, powder, hardpack, crust), rock scrapes, carve and wind layers, varied one-shots, lift sounds.
- Music: four playlists (Ska-punk, Melodic house, Metal, Lofi hip-hop, plus Shuffle all) picked on the main menu or in Settings. Ska-punk is the DavidKBD "Skank the Summer" pack (CC BY 4.0); playlists without files play a procedural jukebox in that style. See `game/public/music/README.md`.
- Menus: main menu (resort picker, snowboard or skis, music), Esc pause menu (resume, restart, map, settings, quit) and a shared settings panel for audio, graphics, controls (two bindings per action, grouped by context, clashes marked; old one-key maps migrate; standard-mapping gamepad support in `sim/Controls.ts`, FEAT-124), rider and game options, saved in localStorage.
- Controllers (FEAT-137): `sim/Pad.ts` is the one source every reader asks (`activePad()`): the Gamepad API on the standard mapping, a Sony pad with no mapping remapped from the raw DirectInput order (Firefox; best effort), or the DualSense over WebHID while it is live (then the Gamepad API copy is skipped so presses don't double up). It detects the kind from the id (Sony vendor `054c`, DualSense `0ce6`, Edge `0df2`, "DualSense" / "Wireless Controller"; Xbox `045e`), holds the button names and `DEFAULT_PAD` (action to two standard button indices; the sticks and the pause buttons Options / PS are fixed). `Controls` feeds the buttons through `held()` / `action()` like the keys (`padKeys`, triggers held past `padCfg.trigDead`), keeps `padOnce` / `padHeld` for the map and the replay viewer, and `padMute()` ignores buttons held through a menu press until they are let go. `ui/PadGlyphs.ts` draws the buttons (our own SVG shapes for the PlayStation face buttons, lettered circles for Xbox, pills for the rest); `main.ts` builds the hints and prompts from them while `controls.usingPad` (a controller is connected and was used after the last key press or mouse click: `padUi.lastWasPad`, set by `Controls` and `PadNav`). `ui/PadNav.ts` runs its own frame loop for the menus (`#menu`, `#pause`, `#outrun-card`): spatial focus, `[data-pad-back]` for Circle, L1 / R1 tabs, `body.pad-on` / `pad-ps` for CSS (`.kb-only`, `.pad-only`, `.pad-hint`), keyboard rebind slots skipped. `sim/PadFeedback.ts` (`padFx`) turns rider events (`landingShock` rising, `crashed`, `surface.rock` / `scree`, `chop` rising), bumps (`jolt`: the rate of change of `vz`, which on the snow is the surface's vertical speed at every 120 Hz step, smoothed over about 70 ms so it reads the same at any frame rate; from `BUMP_A0` m/s2, full `BUMP_A1` above it, times speed up to 8 m/s, half on groomed snow), a held press (a hum, then a beat that quickens toward either end of `pressBalance`: the strong motor toward the slam, the weak one toward dropping flat), trial events and Outrun footfalls into rumble (`vibrationActuator.playEffect('dual-rumble')` renewed every 80 ms, or the HID motors) and drives the lightbar and triggers. `sim/DualSense.ts` is the WebHID driver: report layouts from the Linux `hid-playstation` driver (USB 0x01 / 0x02, Bluetooth 0x31 with the CRC-32 seeded by 0xA2, feature report 0x05 to get full Bluetooth reports, 0x20 for the firmware's rumble style), trigger effects (Feedback 0x21, Weapon 0x25) from Nielk1's notes; the gyro sign and scale (about 16 units per deg/s) feed `Controls.gyroDx / gyroDy`, a touchpad swipe of 550 / 1920 within 0.6 s cycles the camera.
- Touch controls on phones and tablets.

## Code layout

- `game/` the client (Three.js 0.172, TypeScript, Vite). `cd game && npm install && npm run dev`.
  - `src/pack/` pack loader, tiled height / mask sampling, bridge decks
  - `src/terrain/` quadtree LOD terrain and snow material
  - `src/sim/` rider physics, controls (controller source `Pad.ts`, DualSense WebHID `DualSense.ts`, rumble and lights `PadFeedback.ts`), collisions, lift riding, time trials, replay recording, Outrun, run stats (`RunStats.ts`)
  - `src/world/` trees, rocks, buildings, roads, bridges, lifts and lift-line mazes, landmarks, piste overlay, sky, sun, clouds, snow, rider model and ragdoll, cameras, the Outrun monster
  - `src/ui/` main menu, pause menu, settings, resort map, horizon labels, goggles, speed lines, Outrun HUD, touch controls, run card (`RunCard.ts`), press balance meter (`PressMeter.ts`), controller glyphs and menu navigation (`PadGlyphs.ts`, `PadNav.ts`), shared speed / height / distance formatters (`Units.ts`)
  - `src/audio/` synthesised sound and the music player / jukebox
  - `public/music/` soundtrack, `playlist.json`, credits
- `pipeline/` data pipeline (Python, plus PowerShell fetch scripts for Windows)
- `raw/` downloaded source data (gitignored); the Vail download currently lives at `C:\Users\Drew\Documents\snowboard-sim\raw\vail`
- `packs/` generated resort packs (gitignored); the dev server serves them at `/packs/`
- `docs/` plans, handoff, board, research and data notes
- `tools/` repo helpers: `card_id.py` (next free card id, duplicate ids; set `PYTHONUTF8=1` on Windows), `gmaps2game.py` (Street View URL to an eye-camera link)

## Test tools (`game/tools/`)

- `browser.mjs` the shared Chromium launcher every test tool uses (FEAT-146). Real GPU headless by default on Windows, SwiftShader elsewhere; every tool takes `--headed` (window, real GPU), `--gpu` (force the real GPU, exit if it falls back to software) and `--cpu` (force SwiftShader). Prints the renderer on the first line and adds `&lowgfx` automatically on software.
- `shoot.mjs [pack] [outDir] [--headed | --gpu | --cpu]` render and ride test, screenshots into `game/shots/`. Also `npm run shoot` / `npm run shoot:headed`.
- `ridecheck.mjs [pack] [seconds] [--headed]` streaming and determinism check: a straight tuck run ridden twice; reports top speed, late tiles and tree cells, and whether run 2 matches run 1 exactly.
- `rider.mjs [pack] [outDir] [--headed]` rider close-up check: stand, tuck, carves, jump, crash and skis; records the worst boot-to-binding gap and saves side-view shots.
- `walkcheck.mjs [pack] [outDir] [--headed] [--ski]` on-foot check: unstrap, walk, sprint, turn, strap back in; prints speed, stamina, foot-IK gap and bootprint spacing.
- `outruncheck.mjs [pack] [outDir] [--gpu | --headed]` Outrun flow: chase, look back, the eat sequence.
- `views.mjs <pack> <outDir> <views.json> [--headed | --gpu]` stills from fixed free-camera spots for look checks (`views-*.json` in the same folder).
- `shoot_features.mjs` map, buildings, rocks and lift feature check.
- `probe.mjs` step timing probe for hangs.
- `brakecheck.mjs` brake physics on exact planes, no browser: hockey-stop time and distance against the snow-cutting targets, board swing, release, standing across the hill, ski snowplough, and the steer-modulated brake (FEAT-133: a harder stop into the edge, a skidded turn and a side-slip away from it, release onto the new line). Exits 1 on a failed check.
- `carvecheck.mjs` carve feel on exact planes, no browser (BUG-053): turn rate and radius at 25 m/s standing and tucked, edge-to-edge time and the hang at the crossover, turning back up across the hill, the slow pivot. Exits 1 on a failed check.
- `brakeshot.mjs [pack] [outDir] [--headed] [--ski]` hockey-stop render check: tucks to speed, holds brake, saves shots of the swing, spray and scraped track.
- `hardcarvecheck.mjs` hard carve physics on exact planes, no browser (FEAT-148): at 10 and 15 m/s on a groomer a hard carve is much tighter than normal steering and pulls about 2.5 g; committed (on edge before the press) it bites clean, pressed from flat at 15 m/s it skids wider and bleeds more speed, from flat at 20 m/s (45 mph) on a groomer or in tracked-out off-piste chop (not at 15 m/s) and at 15 m/s on ice it washes out to the inside of the turn (toeside right, heelside left); committed it holds in chop and on ice, skidding hard on ice; deep powder planes instead; a squat with no steer is just a squat; linked committed hard carves never wash out and ride slower than the same turns steered normally. Exits 1 on a failed check.
- `hardposecheck.mjs [pack] [--headed]` hard carve pose check, no rendering (BUG-066): steps the physics and the rider view down the fall line on groomed snow and measures, in the board's frame, the skin clearance of the knees, upper shins and thighs over the deck, the deepest lean and the hip height along the body line, toeside and heelside from 12 and 18 m/s; checks at least 5 cm clear, a lean of 60 deg or more, hips at least 0.45 m up the body, and that a straight squat still sits lower. Exits 1 on a failed check.
- `carveshot.mjs [pack] [outDir] [--headed] [--ski] [--hard] [--groomed]` carve track render check (FEAT-109): tucks to speed, links hard carves, saves the knife-cut line close up, overhead and from 40 m down the run, and prints the edge amount per shot. `--hard` holds Shift through each carve once on edge (FEAT-148) and adds chase, side, front and inside shots of the first one; `--groomed` pins the physics to groomed snow wherever the spawn is (the Vail spawn is off-piste powder).
- `trailcheck.mjs [pack] [outDir] [--headed]` deformable snow check (FEAT-044): on a powder day tucks to speed, links two carves and brakes to a stop, saves chase and overhead shots with the deformation on and off (`*_nodeform.png`), reads the deformation texture back and checks a trench at least 4 cm deep sits behind the board, the powder trench is deeper than 8 cm, the stop left a ridge, the terrain chunk count does not change with the deformation on, the capture is skipped while standing still with nothing new stamped, the spray spawned in the carves and the stop, and the console stayed clean. Exits 1 on a failed check.
- `mogulcheck.mjs` (FEAT-059, FEAT-147) the mogul bump function and the Rider over bump fields on exact planes, no browser. The function: bumps sized by the slope (chest to head high at 33 degrees, knee to thigh at 22, small at 17), crest heights that vary, 3 to 9 bumps per 100 m2, a pattern that changes down the run, troughs at the base, the 1.8 m cap, uphill creep, the local anchor cells (BUG-064: a small turn of the fall line keeps the same bumps anywhere on the map, four-cell corners keep their relief), no steps at the cell blend, and the analytic slope the shader uses. The Rider, after a 10 m run-in: thrown off the snow and jolted on a 25 degree field and at least 10% slower than the flat plane beside it, a field that appears under the rider (its tile loading late) neither drops nor flings them, and a 33 degree field of big bumps rides straight down without a crash. Exits 1 on a failed check.
- `presscheck.mjs` press balance physics on exact planes, no browser (FEAT-140): Off holds a press forever, On with no correction loses it within a few seconds (slams off the loaded end, drops flat off the other), a simulated keyboard player holds a groomer press 4 to 10 s and a careful one longer, faster and mogul runs are harder, Easy lasts longer, a lost press stays flat until the key is let go, a slow over-press stalls, a short hop rides through and an ollie out of a press reports it clean, a quick player never loses one (60 s on Hard, fast, on moguls), and a press key held through a trick lands into the press named after it. Prints the median press times. Exits 1 on a failed check.
- `fakiecheck.mjs` riding switch physics on an exact plane, no browser (FEAT-139), regular and goofy: a 180 lands switch with the board drawn turned round and no jump, steering right turns right switch, a switch 360 stays switch and a 180 goes back, sliding back after carving up the hill turns switch still facing the same way, unstrapping and strapping in keep the rider where they face, and the trick names. Exits 1 on a failed check.
- `glancecheck.mjs [pack] [--headed]` look behind (G) in every camera (BUG-057), stepping the physics and the cameras without rendering (seconds on SwiftShader): first person and the helmet cam look back up the hill above level with the run above in the top of the frame; the chase, close, far and cine cameras swing to the downhill side with the rider in the lower part of the frame and the centre of the view on the run 15 m or more above the rider; letting go looks ahead again with no cut. `SHOT_OUT=<dir>` saves a frame. Exits 1 on a failed check.
- `switchcheck.mjs [pack] [--headed]` riding switch in the game (FEAT-139), regular and goofy, stepping the physics and the rider view without rendering (about 30 s even on SwiftShader): lands a 180 and checks the drawn yaw never pops, the rig's toe side flips across the heading, SWITCH on the HUD and the trick toast, the heel (regular) or toe (goofy) edge drawn engaged steering right switch, a 180 back, the head looking ahead along the direction of travel in every stance, and a time-trial ghost riding switch on the snow drawn without errors (BUG-059). `SHOT_OUT=<dir>` saves a frame per stance. Exits 1 on a failed check.
- `leadcheck.mjs [pack] [--headed] [--control]` body lead / board lag check (BUG-056): linked carves on the synthetic mountain; at each edge change prints the ms at which the shoulders' pose, the hips' pose, the body's inclination, the physics edge and the drawn board cross to the new side, and checks that order, that the drawn board never rolls past the physics edge, that the drawn yaw stays near the physics yaw, and that tapping left and right every 133 ms keeps the body smooth (no velocity kinks). `--control` zeroes the leads and the lag to show the old look. Exits 1 on a failed check.
- `snowcheck.mjs [pack] [--headed]` snow conditions check (FEAT-053, Vail spots built in): drops the rider on a groomer, a north face, trees, an open south face, a ridge and a low spring slope at set hours and weather, steps the physics without rendering, and prints the snow kind and weights, the speed reached down the fall line, the carve radius, the skid, the chop rattle and whether a stopped rider gets going again; checks the groomer is Groomed and fast, spring-morning ice holds less and skids, powder turns wider and does not rattle, the afternoon has more chop than the morning, the trees are deep powder, spring afternoon low is slush, and the ridge is firmer. Exits 1 on a failed check.
- `padcheck.mjs [pack] [--headed]` controller check (FEAT-137), no hardware: the DualSense report helpers in Node (CRC-32, USB and Bluetooth output reports with the motors, triggers and lightbar at their offsets, trigger effect bytes, input parsing of buttons, hat, sticks, triggers, touchpad and gyro), then on a stubbed `navigator.getGamepads` DualSense the main menu (glyph hint, focus on Free ride, D-pad, Cross into Settings, R1 tab, Circle back) and a headless session (R2 past the trigger dead zone, Cross jump and release, one map press per D-pad press, Options pause, the stick dead zone, a rebound button, a muted button, PlayStation glyphs in the corner hints and keys again after a key press, rumble on a landing and a crash, vibration 0 off), and on a scripted rider the press rumble (hum when centred, the heavy motor near the slam, the light one near dropping flat) and bumps (still on a smooth slope, rumbling at speed, softer at a crawl and half on groomed snow). Exits 1 on a failed check.
- `leancheck.mjs [pack] [outDir] [--headed] [--ski]` carve lean check (FEAT-126): a gentle turn, then hard linked carves; prints the lean, hip height and each body part's lowest point above the snow, and saves shots at the deepest toeside and heelside lean.
- `shoot_spots.mjs [pack] [spot ...] [--gpu | --headed]` re-shoots the game side of the real vs game photo pairs in `docs/compare/<pack>/` with the eye camera, as `eye-<view>.png` (DATA-008; see [compare/README.md](compare/README.md)).
- `host.mjs [--https] [--port N]` (`npm run host`) hosts the game for other computers on the home network (FEAT-138): `vite build` into `game/dist-host/` (not `dist/`, which the test tools serve), then `vite preview --host` on port 5173 with `../packs` served at `/packs/` (the `servePacks` plugin has a `configurePreviewServer` hook next to `configureServer`), and prints the LAN URLs with `?pack=vail`. The build sets `VITE_HOST_NAME` to the PC's name: the main menu shows "Hosted from <PC> · <address>", and the hosted build leaves out the dev-only tools (`?debug` is ignored, P in the eye camera downloads the shot instead of posting to `/__compare/save`, which only the dev server has). `--https` or `HOST_HTTPS=1` adds `@vitejs/plugin-basic-ssl` (a self-signed certificate cached in `node_modules/.vite/basic-ssl`, renewed after 30 days) so the page is a secure context: Firefox, and Chrome as it phases the rule in, only expose the Gamepad API there. `sim/Pad.ts` reads gamepads through `readPads()`, which returns null and stops asking if `getGamepads` throws, so plain http falls back to the keyboard.
- `fetch-music.ps1 -Source <folder> -Playlist <name>` converts downloaded tracks into a playlist (needs ffmpeg).

Typecheck before committing: `cd game && npx tsc --noEmit`.

## Pipeline scripts (`pipeline/`)

Flow: `resorts/<slug>.json` -> `gen_manifest.py` -> `manifests/<slug>.json` -> `fetch.ps1` -> `raw/<slug>/` -> `build_pack.py` -> `packs/<slug>/`.

- `resorts/<slug>.json` resort config (origin, UTM bbox, rings, NAIP items, lidar nodes).
- `gen_manifest.py` writes `manifests/<slug>.json`, the download list (needs `pip install pyproj`).
- `fetch.ps1` resumable Windows downloader (about 1.4 GB for Vail; `-RawDir` picks the destination), launched by `fetch_vail.cmd` in the repo root. Run one fetch at a time.
- `fetch_static.ps1` (launcher `fetch_static.cmd`) Geofabrik Colorado OSM extract and USGS GNIS names into `raw/static/`.
- `fetch_landfire.mjs <slug> <email>` (launcher `fetch_landfire.cmd`) LANDFIRE EVT clip via the LFPS job service into `raw/<slug>/landfire/`.
- `fetch_grooming.py <slug> <raw_dir>` the resort's real grooming history (Vail's nightly reports, from Internet Archive snapshots of its terrain status page) into `raw/<slug>/grooming/`. Groomed runs in the pack come only from these reports and OSM `piste:grooming` tags.
- `extract_osm.py` cuts `osm/*.json` and `names/gnis.json` out of the static files (needs `pip install osmium`), the Overpass-free route for future resorts.
- `build_pack.py <slug> <raw_dir> <out_dir>` raw inputs to a resort pack (`pip install -r pipeline/requirements.txt`), about 4 minutes and 234 MB for Vail.
- `synth_pack.py <out_dir>` synthetic test mountain in the same format, for client work without real data.
- `packwriter.py` shared pack writer (Terrarium height tiles, masks) used by `build_pack.py` and `synth_pack.py`.
- `terrain_ao.py` baked sky visibility (terrain ambient occlusion) from the DEM, used by the pack build.
- `piste_dir.py` piste direction (DATA-002): the groomed runs' axis per 2 m cell as the `dir/` tile set, used by both pack builds; `python pipeline/piste_dir.py packs/<slug>` adds it to an existing pack from `features.json` and the mask tiles without a rebuild. The terrain (`Terrain.ts`) points it downhill and feeds it to the corduroy as the `fall` attribute, falling back to the smoothed fall line where a pack has no set.
- `moguls.py` (FEAT-059) scores each run for mogul fields (grooming reports, difficulty, slope and aspect along the centre line, lift-top traffic; cat tracks, glades and greens never) and paints the amplitude raster the pack build writes as the `moguls` tile set. The build log lists the top-scoring runs. Tests: `cd pipeline && python -m pytest tests`.
- `ept_select.py <dataset> <depth_in_ski_area> <depth_in_buffer> [out.txt]` picks the EPT lidar nodes over a resort for the manifest (boxes are hard-coded for Breckenridge).
- `prep_textures.py [work_dir]` fetches the CC0 ambientCG rock and talus sets and writes the game's surface textures into `game/public/textures/`.

The data hosts (USGS, Planetary Computer, Overpass, Mapterhorn) are not reachable from cloud sandboxes; fetching runs on Drew's PC. Before touching raw data read [data/vail-raw-data-inventory.md](data/vail-raw-data-inventory.md). Pack layout: [pack-format.md](pack-format.md).

## URL parameters

- `pack=<slug>` resort pack to load (`vail`, `breckenridge`, `synth`)
- `x=&y=&h=` spawn in local metres with heading in degrees
- `cam=fp` start in first person
- `cam=eye&pitch=&fov=&eye=&spot=` still camera at eye height (default 1.7 m) over `x, y`, looking along `h` with `pitch` degrees up (negative is down) and `fov` vertical degrees; HUD and rider hidden, P saves the shot (into `docs/compare/` when `spot=<slug>/<view>` is set). Make these links from a Street View URL with `python tools/gmaps2game.py`.
- `time=now` or `time=16.5` override the time of day
- `weather=clear|cirrus|few|scattered|broken|overcast|flurries|storm` override the cloud cover; `valleyfog` forces valley fog on
- `rider=ski` or `rider=board`
- `stance=regular` or `stance=goofy` (FEAT-139, overrides Settings > Rider > Stance)
- `debug=pad` (FEAT-137, also in the `npm run host` build) an overlay of every connected controller: id, mapping, buttons held with their values, axes, whether it can rumble, the WebHID DualSense's transport, report count, battery, gyro and touch, and what the game reads
- `debug` (not in the `npm run host` build) adds a HUD line with the edge angle, lateral acceleration, turn radius and turn rate, a line with the snow condition weights (FEAT-053), and sliders for the body lead / board lag chain (BUG-056)
- `nomoguls` flattens the mogul fields (FEAT-059); with `debug`, the resort map (M) under-strokes runs scoring 0.3 or more in orange and the hover line shows the nearest run's score. `debug=moguls` (BUG-062) tints the terrain by mogul amplitude: green where the bumps are in the chunk geometry (1 and 2 m chunks), red where the shader alone draws them (4 to 16 m), faint magenta on chunks too coarse for either
- `lowgfx` reduced graphics for software GPUs; `headless` is used by the test tools; `csm` / `nocsm` force cascaded shadows on or off; `noimpostors` disables tree impostors; `nodeform` turns the snow deformation off (FEAT-044, for A/B shots); `bridgeguess` adds the segment-chord bridge detector

## Milestones

M0 pipeline (Vail) -> M1 terrain viewer -> M2 riding physics -> M3 trees and rock -> M4 lifts and landmarks -> M5 snow and atmosphere -> M6 second resort, timed runs, multiplayer.

M0 to M3 are done on the real Vail pack. M4 has rideable lifts, buildings, roads, bridges and labels. M5 has time of day, clouds, snowstorms and snow tracks. M6 has started: Breckenridge is playable from the resort picker, and time trials with ghosts are in. Open work is on the board.
