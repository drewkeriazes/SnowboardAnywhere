## Features in detail

- Terrain: quadtree LOD over the 262 km far ring, 1 m near the rider, snow / rock / scree / groomed material; forested ground darkens from the tree impostor ring outward so the far forest reads dark. Distant forest (FEAT-153, `pack/CoverSet.ts`): past about 300 m the shader takes the forest share per pixel from the pack's cover levels (`cover/*.png`, a mipmapped texture per level, up to 3), so the real forest edges and the ski trails cut through it show at any distance; to 1.2 km it reads the level's closed stands of drawn trees (G of the texture, the `stands` image) and from 2.8 km the forest as a mass (R), so open slopes with a few trees stay white up close and far timber stays solid (BUG-069); chunks coarser than 8 m take forest, rock and canopy height from the same levels per vertex instead of the procedural treeline (`proceduralForest`, now only where no level reaches or before they load). From the impostors' reach (`uLiftNear`, Vegetation's FAR_RADIUS: 4.5 km at full tree density, nearer on lower density settings) to 2 km past it, chunks coarser than 8 m rise by canopy height x cover x 0.7 (the `lift` attribute, applied in the vertex shader) so forested ridges read as treed; the far timber has a crown-scale texture that fades to an even tone once a pixel is wider than a crown. Forest from high up (FEAT-154, `docs/research/far-terrain.md` section 7): how dark forested ground goes is a gap fraction, `1 - ((1 - s) / (1 - rho s))^((1 + 2.5 sqrt(1 - mu^2)) / mu)` of the cover share `s` and the cosine `mu` between the ground normal and the view (the 2.5, `TREE_ASPECT`, is a conifer's side silhouette over its plan area: tall trees close the gaps sooner in a slanted view, BUG-069), so grazing views from the snow see a closed mass and views from the air see the openings; inside the trees' reach `rho` (`uForestDrawn`, 0.7) is the part of the canopy the drawn trees already cover, and outside the pack's tree instances (`uTreeRect`, set by `Terrain` from `meta.instances`) the ground carries the forest from the first metre. Sunlit canopy takes `uCanopySun` (0.22) of the direct light and a forested pixel is `uTimberMix` (0.97) timber. The ridges round the resort (BUG-081): the pack's trees and the far tree ring's (`farTrees/`, loaded with the same cells, `treeRing` cells past the grid) draw as impostors out to 9 km at full tree density (`FAR_RADIUS`, twice the thinning start). Past 4.5 km (`THIN_RADIUS`) an impostor stays with chance (4.5 km / d)^2 and grows by d / 4.5 km (`THIN_GLSL` in `world/trees/materials.ts`), so the ground under crowns holds while the count falls; `Vegetation.packFar` leaves the ones that can't show out of the buffers (the draw is per tree, packed into `iSpec`'s fraction). Past the generated stems' reach (`vegetationStemReach`, near radius + 1 km) the impostors grow by `STEM_GROW` (2.2, the square root of the stems' 4.2x extra canopy on Vail) to stand in for the stems, and the terrain lets them carry 0.92 of the forest (`FOREST_DRAWN_FAR`, `uStemReach`) instead of laying flat timber between them; both only on packs with stands. The Low preset keeps one 600 m reach. Open ground far off (BUG-081): from 900 m (full by 2.2 km), ungroomed snow where the season's snowpack is thinner than 0.25 to 1 m shows the sage, grass and low shrub poking through it, up to 0.6 of a pixel in 40 m and 12 m patches, so low sun-facing slopes read darker than the north-facing resort side. The depth is FEAT-159's (`sim/Snowpack.ts`: month, elevation, aspect, new snow; `SNOWPACK_GLSL` is the same formula for the shader, set through `Terrain.setSnowpack` next to the shrubs' state). Tree shadows past the shadow map (FEAT-168, FEAT-171): the sun's shadow camera reaches about 260 m around the rider and only the LOD0 / LOD1 trees (to 400 m) cast into it, so past that the terrain shader takes the direct sun off the snow itself (the blue sky fill stays, so the shade is blue). Near and mid distance it reads the shade map (`world/TreeShade.ts`, owned by `Vegetation`): every drawn tree's shadow as one quad with a cone's outline (the crown-base disc joined to the tip's shadow, from the tree's height, crown radius and species: `crownBase`, and bare aspen and snags let more sun through), laid from the trunk away from the sun and stretched by the ground slope under the tree (from a 20 m height grid per cell, `Pack.demHeight`), drawn from above into a three-layer array target around the camera (half-widths 512, 1024 and 2048 m at 2048 px, so 0.5, 1 and 2 m texels; 1024 px with `?lowgfx`). A layer is redrawn, at most one per frame, when the camera has moved a fifth of its half-width, when the sun has turned 0.25 degrees, or when a cell of trees arrives or leaves; the terrain picks the layer whose texel is about a pixel on the ground. Where the shade map doesn't reach, `treeShadow` steps 8 times toward the sun through the pack cover level's map of where trees stand (B of that level's texture, `uTreeMap`; from the `stands` image's G, 4 m cells), over `TREE_SHADOW_H` (12 m) / tan(sun elevation), capped at 48 m, but only where one of its cells is under about 2 to 3 pixels (about 1.5 km and out at 1080p), so it never paints soft patches nearer. The handover to the shadow map (`Terrain.setTreeShadeGate`, set per frame in `RideFrame`) is at the LOD1 edge and outside the sun's shadow camera (its shadow matrix), from the camera distance only with cascades on, and from 200 m (full by 320 m) with shadows off. None in flat light or on the canopy. `uDebugFar` switches terms off for A/B stills (x forest, y rock and talus, z snow drift normals; w paints the forest darkening red), for example `SHOT_EVAL` or a `tools/aerial.mjs` variant setting `__sim.terrain.material.userData.uniforms.uDebugFar.value.set(1, 0, 0, 0)`.
- Trees (FEAT-156, `world/trees/`, drawn by `world/Vegetation.ts`): a procedural asset kit, nothing downloaded. `species.ts` is the table of the eight species (ids as in the pack, DATA-020: 0 Engelmann spruce, 1 aspen, 2 krummholz, 3 subalpine fir, 4 lodgepole, 5 Douglas-fir, 6 limber pine, 7 snag) with their shape, colours, whorls and variant weights; packs without `meta.treeSpecies` (0 to 2 only) split their conifers 60/40 into spruce and fir by a position hash. `textures.ts` draws one 1024 x 1024 atlas at load (12 grey branch-spray cells and 8 bark swatches, tinted per species by vertex colour; transparent texels carry the cell's mean colour and each mip keeps the cell's alpha coverage). `generate.ts` builds every species x 4 variants (seeded: tier count, gaps, droop, lean, a double or broken top on some spruce and fir) at unit height and crown radius 1 from one plan per variant, so LOD0 (trunk + bent alpha cards, normals bent out from the trunk) and LOD1 (trunk + star-edged cones or blobs) share a silhouette. `materials.ts` holds the LOD hook: each LOD draws only in its band of distance from the camera (`kitUniforms.uLodCam`, so the shadow pass agrees) and crossfades with the next by a screen-door dither (Bayer 8x8, complementary cells); LOD0 sways (`TreeKit.setWind`). LOD2 (crossed quads) and the impostors (camera-facing quads) sample albedo and normal atlases baked from LOD0 (`TreeKit.bake`, 8 angles x 8 species) and are lit in the shader like the standard material (sun, hemisphere, a quarter of the GGX specular, `FAR_GAIN`), so the colour holds across the swaps. Bands: LOD0 to 80 m, LOD1 to 400 m, LOD2 to 1.5 km, impostors to 4.5 km, scaled by the tree density setting (`setVegetationDensity`). `Vegetation` fills LOD0 and LOD1 from the trees near the camera (one InstancedMesh per species x variant, regathered every 25 / 60 m of camera movement through a 100 m sub-tile index per cell) and keeps one LOD2 and one impostor mesh per 1 km cell (all species, `iSpec` per instance). Culling (FEAT-158): before each draw `Vegetation.cull(camera, sun)` (Session's `draw`) keeps only the near sets' 25 m (LOD0) / 50 m (LOD1) tiles in view, or whose trees' shadows can reach into it (each tile's box stretched away from the sun by its tallest tree's shadow, up to 200 m), at the front of the instance buffers, and of those the tiles inside the sun's shadow camera first: the shadow pass draws only that prefix (`onBeforeShadow` swaps `mesh.count`), and of each LOD1 tree only the triangles that cast (a star cone's underside sits inside its shadow; `generate.ts` puts those last and `geometry.userData.castCount` says where they start). A set repacks only when a tile it needs is missing, with 15 / 40 m of slack so turning repacks a few times a second at most. A far mesh holds only the 100 m tiles of its cell within 150 m of its band and repacks once the camera has moved enough for the band to reach a tile it left out, so the cells round the camera no longer draw their every tree as a collapsed quad. The Low preset draws LOD1 from the camera out (no LOD0: `setVegetationBudget(near, far, false)`). Stand density (FEAT-074): a pack with `meta.stands` holds only the detected crowns, and a cell within 1 km past the near radius (1.5 km at full density, so 2.5 km) loads its `stands/<cx>_<cy>.png` and appends the stems it asks for (`trees/stands.ts`, `generateStems`: a position hash per 4 m cell, so the same stems every time) to its records, then rebuilds its LODs and shade entry. So they draw at every near LOD and as impostors in the mid ring, and nothing beyond, where the terrain's cover (which counts them) shades the forest. A cell drops them 250 m past that edge. The Forest setting (`forestDensity`, `setStandDensity`, scaled by auto quality) thins only these stems; the detected crowns always draw. Generated stems are trunks in the collider only in the cells within 150 m of the rider. `vegetation.stats.stems` counts them. Tune the look with `tools/treeshot.mjs`, the cost with `tools/treeperf.mjs`.
- Shrubs and understory (FEAT-159, `world/Shrubs.ts`, geometry in `world/trees/shrubs.ts`): the pack's shrub set (DATA-020, `shrubs/<cx>_<cy>.bin`: 0 willow, 1 juniper, 2 sagebrush, 3 young conifer, 4 Gambel oak) drawn within 300 m of the camera (scaled with the tree density setting) with the tree kit's atlas and material (`treeMaterial(..., { shrub: true })`: one more program, `SHRUB` defined). Willow is 10 to 12 straight red-orange stems as crossed strips (48 triangles), juniper a wide low dark dome and sagebrush a round grey-green one (36), Gambel oak 3 or 4 crooked grey stems each with a twig spray (40); young conifers are the kit's spruce or fir LOD1 (90), a little bushier than grown trees. One InstancedMesh per kind x variant, refilled from the shrubs in reach every 25 m of camera movement or when the snowpack changes; the band dithers them out at the edge. Burial: the terrain is the snow surface, so each shrub is sunk by the snowpack depth at its spot (`sim/Snowpack.ts`: a settled base by month, 1.45 m at 3,000 m in February, scaled by elevation from a third on the I-70 valley floor to 1.25 times from 3,200 m, less on steep south faces and more on north ones, plus the new snow and a fresh day's loose layer; +-10 % per shrub) and only what stands above it is drawn (`buryShrub`). A buried juniper, sagebrush or young conifer still shows a snow-covered hump of up to 0.25 to 0.4 m, fading out under 2 m more snow; willow and oak just go under. Per instance `aShrub` carries the snow line, where the material drifts snow round the stems (5 to 25 cm up), and the mound cover for a half-buried mat or sapling; the visible tops take the tree snow (FEAT-157) and no snow ghosts. Physics: a young conifer standing over 1.5 m above the snow gets a trunk collider like a tree; the rest is brush (`Collider.addBrush`, updated in place as the snow changes): `rider.surface.shrub` (0..1) adds `SHRUB_DRAG` (rider/tuning.ts, about 0.5 m/s through a willow clump at 12 m/s) and the brush sound (`Audio.shrubBrush`). Look with `treeshot.mjs --only=shrubs`, physics with `shrubcheck.mjs`, depth on the spot with `?snowdepth=`.
- Snow on the trees (FEAT-157, `world/trees/snow.ts`): one load for the day (`treeSnowState`, set from `RideFrame.streaming` through `TreeKit.setSnow`): 0.55 / 0.35 / 0.12 for fresh / mid-season / spring snow conditions, scaled by how deep in the winter the month is, rising to 1 with the shared new-snow cover (FEAT-105) after a storm; `melt` takes it back on the sun side of each crown from late morning on a clear day (much more in spring); `ghost` is 1 in deep winter. Each tree varies the load by +-15%, and above the snow-ghost band (`uGhostZ`, 3,450 to 3,560 m at treeline, jittered per tree) a tree goes to a full load with rime on every face and its cards' alpha grown so the gaps fill in (its shadow fills in too). In LOD0 and LOD1 the snow sits on the upper side of what you see (the surface's own facing from screen derivatives, so the top of a card is white and its underside stays green, leaning on the crown's bent normal), times the vertex's `snowHold` from `generate.ts` (full at the base of a bough, 0.4 at its tip, none under the cones or on bark) and more on the lower tiers, broken up by a 0.3 m noise whose threshold falls as the load rises; LOD1's cones take 0.6 of the amount (`LOD1_SNOW`) so they match the cards. The bake adds a snow mask atlas (cover at load 0.5, at 1, on a ghost, and the ghost's filled silhouette), and the far shader blends bare, moderate and heavy by the tree's load. Snow uses the terrain's albedo and shade tint (`world/snowLook.ts`, shared with the tracks, the rider's snow cover and `TerrainMaterial`) with its normal pulled toward up. Needles get a fill from the snow under the crown and a back-lit translucency (`treeNeedleExtra`), so shaded trees read dark blue-green. No extra draw calls; texture memory 21.3 MB with the mask.
- Snow off the boughs (FEAT-217, `world/TreeBrush.ts`): each frame (view sync, render pose; not on a lift or with the camera detached) `Vegetation.crownContact` finds the needled crown the rider's body is deepest in: three heights on the body (0.3, 0.9, 1.6 m) against the generator's crown profile (`crownProfile`, from the species' crown base to the top), 0.35 m half width, full contact 0.8 m in; aspen and snags hold no snow. The first touch of a tree drops up to 45 % of its near-side snow, riding on through it shakes more down with depth and speed, standing still drops nothing. The amount is the tree's load as the shader draws it (`treeSnowLoadAt` in `trees/snow.ts`: the day's load +-15 % or the snow-ghost load; nothing under 0.15, so none on a spring day) less half the sun-side melt, times the crown size; a tree remembers what it shed (the last 256) and builds back over 4 minutes; a replay or the rewind playing back shakes the trees without taking their snow (a memory of its own), so it drops what the live pass did. The snow goes out through the board's spray ring (`Spray.emit` / `commit`, no draw call of its own, at most 96 a frame): clumps (60 %) that fall from the contact height to 1.6 m above it on the near side of the crown and a fine cloud that hangs and drifts on the wind, both carried a little along with the rider; a touch also plays a soft thump and the hiss of it sifting down (`GameAudio.treePuff`). The unload: each frame the 8 shaken trees nearest the rider go to the tree shader (`uTreeShed` / `uTreeShedN` in `trees/snow.ts`: the base, the share of the near side gone, the contact height and the near side's direction; uniforms, so no per-instance data). The mesh LODs' vertex shader matches its instance's base against them (`treeSnowShed`, skipped when none) and the fragment shader takes the load off the near side, from 1.4 m under the contact height to about 2.5 m over it, soft at the edges; the bare side fills in as the tree builds back. A ghost's rime goes there too, though its shadow keeps the filled crown. The far LODs (past 400 m) don't show it. A slam: riding into a trunk (`Rider.lastHit` 'wall' or 'graze', with `Rider.hitSpeed` the speed into it, cleared each frame with `lastHit`) shakes the whole tree, which `Vegetation.trunkAt` finds (the nearest base within its collider radius plus 0.7 m, so a lodgepole's high crown counts too). From 2.5 m/s some of it comes off, from 7 m/s (where a square hit crashes) all of it: the tree is drawn bare on every side top to bottom (the second `uTreeShed` vec4's z), and its snow, in all four times a near side's, comes off the whole crown over the next frames at most 96 a frame, the clumps living long enough to fall to the snow. Checked by `treepuffcheck.mjs`.
- Riding: carving on a real edge angle (up to 72 deg on firm snow, 45 in powder) with the turn radius from the board's sidecut, washing into a skid past the grip; edge to edge takes about 0.3 s at speed; the rider leans into the hill up to 60 deg with deep toeside and heelside carve poses. Hard carve (FEAT-148, the squat key held while steering): `Rider.hardCarve` eases in, the edge cap goes to `HARD_PSI` (80 deg) and the edge rolls `HARD_ROLL` times faster (slowing with the square of the speed past `HARD_ROLL_V`), the lean may reach `HARD_PHI` and the snow holds up to `HARD_G` (2.6 g) times its grip; the press asks for the turn of the target edge, driven in over `HARD_DRIVE_T` once the edge is on the steer's side, while the edge holds only what its real angle balances, so a lagging edge (pressed from flat at speed, on ice) skids (`carveShort`, folded into `skid` for the drag, spray and sound) and short of more than `WASH_SHARE` (35%) of the turn asked, at speed, fills `washout` (a share, so softer snow that asks less washes out alike; the snow's grip scales what the edge holds; only deep powder, its share times depth over `HARD_DEEP` 30 cm, lowers the hard carve's edge cap and stops the washout); full, the rider crashes to the inside of the turn (`crashSide`, the ragdoll topples that way) and `onWashout` toasts it. `HARD_DRAG` adds a drag from the lateral acceleration. The view lays the body over to `LEAN_HARD` (73 deg) in the deep carve stance (BUG-066: the squat pose fades out while `hardCarve` is in, and `RiderView.deckDeficit` lifts the hips until the shins, knees and thighs clear the deck in the board's own frame, `DECK_CLEAR`) and drags the inside hand on the snow; the spray, the track depth and the carve hiss rise with it; `?debug` shows hard, short and wash. Also tuck, brake (the board swings across into a hockey stop, scrapes a wide track and holds on its edge once stopped; steering while braking digs the edge in for a harder stop or lets it off into a skidded turn or a side-slip), the fall line (a flat board follows its slide down the hill and side-slips when let go across it unless the uphill edge is held; on Realistic the edge catches when the board slides sideways onto its leading edge, or slides flat over rough snow; Settings > Gameplay > Fall line: Realistic (the default), Assisted, Off; the Feel presets set it, Arcade to Off), charged jumps with ollie and nollie off the board's spring (hold W / S with Space), nose and tail presses (Q / Z) balanced on a meter (FEAT-140: `Rider.pressStep`, the needle `pressBalance` drifts with the hold time, speed and bumps, runs away off centre, and W / S or the stick shift the weight back (over 0.15 s, and always strong enough to win, so there is no time limit); off the loaded end a slam that topples the ragdoll over that end (`Rider.crashFall`) or a stall, off the other end the board drops flat; a press key held through a trick lands into the press (`pressFrom`); `onPress` reports the time and distance, `ui/PressMeter.ts` draws the meter over the head, tunables `PB_*` and `PRESS_RANGE` (how far the balance runs before it is lost; the body leans in proportion) at the top of `Rider.ts`, Settings > Gameplay > Press balance Easy / Normal / Hard / Off, also set by the Feel presets), squat (Left Ctrl), skating on the flat (X), grabs, air spins, front flips and backflips (Up / Down in the air, trick names toast on landing: flips, and spins as "Frontside 180" / "Backside 360"), landings; collisions with trees, rocks and building walls. The snowboard bends when loaded. Snowboard or skis, outfit colours, articulated rider with blended poses. The body leads and the board follows (BUG-056, visual only): the head and shoulders pose about 0.2 s ahead of the physics (the steer's target edge and the heading the turn rate is reaching), the hips and the body's inclination about 0.12 s ahead, each lead signal followed through a critically damped 0.09 s body response (`BODY_RESPONSE`) so the body moves at a human speed instead of snapping to its prediction, and the board as drawn trails the physics yaw and edge by about 0.08 s (critically damped, never rolled past the physics edge, snapped on landings and crashes), so an edge change reads look, shoulders, hips, knees, board. Tunables `LEAD_UPPER`, `LEAD_HIPS`, `BOARD_LAG` at the top of `RiderView.ts`, live on `riderView.leadUpper` / `leadHips` / `boardLag` and as sliders under `?debug`.
- Riding switch and stance (FEAT-139): `rider.heading` is always the steering axis along the direction of travel, and every physics value (edge, lateral g, lean, flip, brake side) is in its frame, so + edge is the heading's right-hand edge. Going backwards along it faster than `FAKIE_SPEED` (2.5 m/s), or landing with the board backwards, `turnRound()` adds PI to the heading and board yaw, negates the edge, lean and lateral g, and toggles `rider.switch`. `rider.goofy` comes from Settings > Gameplay > Stance. `rider.toeSign` is -1 when switch and goofy differ: `RiderView` then draws the board and body at the yaw + PI and negates every sideways and fore-aft value into the rig's frame (`fl`), so turning round never moves the rider on screen and the toe and heel poses stay the rider's own. A goofy rig (`buildRig(..., goofy)`) has the deck's nose at +Z and the +15 / -6 bindings swapped ends, so drawn turned round it is a true goofy stance. Seated and on foot are always drawn along the heading; unstrapping and strapping in turn the heading round when needed so nothing pops. Switch is 4 % less grip (`SWITCH_GRIP`), shows SWITCH on the HUD, and prefixes trick names ("Switch backside 180"). Replays and ghosts carry flag bits 8 (switch) and 16 (goofy). Skis never ride switch.
- Snow conditions (FEAT-053, `sim/SnowConditions.ts`): `snowAt()` turns the surface mask, the ground normal (slope and aspect), the elevation, the canopy and the day (`rider.snowWorld`: hour, storm, new snow cover, the Snow conditions setting, the pack's elevation range, set by `main.ts` each frame) into weights for groomed / powder / chop / ice / slush / rock that sum to 1, plus the physics they blend (`grip`, `drag0`, `drag1`, `turn`, `jolt`, `skidScrub`, `depth`). Powder keeps in the shade of north aspects, high up, under the trees and after a storm (a storm or a powder day makes the groomers ride soft too); open off-piste is tracked out into chop as the day goes on; wind scours high exposed ground to ice and spring mornings refreeze the groomers; the afternoon sun makes slush low down on the sunny faces (most of all in spring). In `Rider.ts` the state scales the grip (ice about half), the sidecut's arc and the lean's turn force (powder 0.7: wider, slower turns), the snow drag (powder speed-scaled until the board planes at `PLANE_SPEED`, the nose sinking and digging in below `SINK_SPEED`, slush heavy), how much a skid scrubs speed (ice little), and chop adds seeded random jolts to the heading and a sideways kick (`CHOP_*`, `rider.chop` for the camera). `bootSink` reads the depth on foot. The spray (`RiderView.updateSpray`: a plume off the edge in powder, almost none on ice), the camera rumble, the track depth and the sound (`Audio.update`: ice on the hardpack layer, slush a wet hush, chop a crackle; footsteps too) follow the weights. The HUD names the dominant kind while riding and shows the kind and the boot sink on foot; `?debug` adds the weights. FEAT-221 (`ui/Conditions.ts`, `conditionsText()` from `ui/HudLine.ts`) turns the riding tag into a plain-words readout: the kind, the depth for powder (and for chop or slush past 10 cm) in 2 in steps (5 cm in metric), `Moguls 4 ft` in front on a bump field (`surface.moguls` 0.3 or more, the size `mogulSize()` at the slope, eased over 1.5 s so the bump faces do not swing it, 0.5 ft or 0.1 m steps), and at most two tags, first match wins: edge letting go (`washout`), bogging down (powder, under 0.6 x `PLANE_SPEED`), nose sinking (`noseSink`), floating (powder at `PLANE_SPEED` or more), edges skittering (`snow.grip` under 0.75), bumpy (`snow.jolt`), heavy and slow (slush), catchy (`catchRisk`), brushing shrubs (`surface.shrub`), in the trees (`surface.canopy`). New text only replaces the shown text once it has held for 0.5 s (a module-level hold; a gap in the calls starts over). The thresholds are in the `TAGS` table at the top of the file.
- Crashes: a Verlet ragdoll takes over the skeleton, tumbles against the snow, trunks, rocks and walls, follows the sim's crash slide and blends back to the animated pose as the rider gets up.
- On foot: B unstraps when nearly stopped (or straps back in standing still); walk with the board carried (on the shoulder when sprinting), sprint on Shift (stamina), step up and climb onto platforms, bootprints in the snow.
- Snow: 3D tracks pressed into the snow behind the rider (carves narrow to a knife-cut line); tracks and bootprints fill in faster while it snows, and new snow buries terrain rock from its edges in.
- Deformable snow and powder spray (FEAT-044, design in `docs/research/deformable-snow-and-spray.md`): a hybrid. The sharp trench is the ribbon (`Tracks.ts`): in soft snow its floor is 0.7 of the loose snow depth (8 to 25 cm in powder, the 5 cm groove on a groomer) and the knife-cut collapse is held back, so a powder carve leaves a trench the board's width, never wider. `world/Deform.ts` renders the track lanes and the boot prints top-down every frame into a 512 px render target over a 64 m window that follows the rider (leading along the travel direction), with MAX blending (R depression, G pushed-up snow, B packed); the lanes and prints are the persistence, so the texture holds no state. Shaders read it only through its mipmap at about 1 m per texel (`deformSag`), so the GPU pre-blurs the stamp to the terrain's 1 m vertex spacing and it cannot alias: the terrain (`TerrainMaterial.ts`), the ribbon and the boot prints all sink by that soft sag at their vertices, and the terrain tilts its normal by the sag's slope. A first build displaced the terrain from the full-resolution texture on a 25 cm split; it beaded along diagonal tracks and cost about 120 draw calls, so it was dropped. `world/Spray.ts` replaces the CPU spray: a ring of 8,192 GPU particles moved analytically in the vertex shader from spawn records (wisps on groomers and ice, clouds that hang, grow and drift on the wind in powder and slush, a low sheet in a hockey stop, a burst on landing), emission from speed x sin(edge angle) x loose depth, the skid and the scrape. Settings > Graphics > Snow dents turns the sag off; the Low preset and `?nodeform` turn it off too; auto quality halves the texture at level 3 and turns it off at level 4, and scales the spray count with the snowfall share.
- Board and boots set down into loose snow (FEAT-152, `world/SnowCover.ts`, `RiderView.setDown`): from 5 cm of loose snow (`rider.snow.depth`; all in by 10 cm, `buryGate`, so groomers at 2.5 cm, ice and rock are untouched and any powder, 12 cm or more, is all in) the board is drawn tail low and nose up (`buryShape`): the tail sinks 0.8 of the depth when slow, 0.45 by `PLANE_SPEED` and no more than 4 cm by 22 m/s, never past 0.9 of it; the nose floats at 0.1 of the depth when slow and at the surface once planing, up to 12 deg nose-up; slow, the nose dig (`noseSink`) takes the nose down to the tail and past it. The rider is lowered by the board's middle along the snow normal and pitched about it, easing in and out over about 0.3 s (up out of it in the air, snapped back on a chair or in a crash). Visual only: the physics, tracks, cameras and spray keep the snow surface. Every rider material gets a snow-cover term (`addSnowCover`): below a plane through the untouched snow under the rider, the fragment takes the snow's albedo, roughness and normal, with a noisy edge from 2 cm under to 4 cm over the surface; the terrain hides what is deeper. The deformation sag is held back ahead of and beside the buried board (`setDeformHold`, from `riderView.powderHold` in `main.ts`), so the snow wall around the nose and the boots stays while the trench opens behind. `Spray.deepPowder` throws a bow wave off the nose at speed and spills snow over the tail when slow. Presses in deep powder (scaled by powder x depth/0.3): in `Rider` a lean forward digs the nose in more and for longer (`SINK_NOSE`, `SINK_NOSE_SPEED`) and a lean back lifts it out (`SINK_TAIL`); the press drag is 1.5 times more on a nose press and gone on a tail press (`PD_NOSE_DRAG`, `PD_TAIL_EASE`); the press balance pulls a nose press on toward its nose (`PB_POWDER`) and gives a tail press less drift, jitter and runaway (`PB_POWDER_STEADY`, `PB_POWDER_CALM`). The press tip and bend apply on top of the set-down, so a tail press lifts the nose out of the cover; the held sag moves 0.35 m toward the end that is down (`HOLD_SHIFT`); the spray follows it: a tail press cuts the bow wave and throws a rooster tail off the tail, a nose press throws more bow wave and spills over the nose. On foot the soles sit down by the whole boot sink in loose snow (`GaitInput.soleShare`, 0.6 of it on firm snow) and the snow covers the boots and shins. The bow wave and the spill are powder only, smaller in shallow powder.
- World: instanced forests (species from LANDFIRE, impostors at distance), rocks with snow caps, extruded buildings, roads and catwalks draped on the terrain, bridges over gaps the bare-earth lidar removes, piste overlay, peak and town labels on the horizon (hidden behind nearer ridges). Landmarks (FEAT-132) are hand-placed signs OSM does not carry, such as Vail's Legendary Back Bowls gateway arch; its name toasts as you ride under it, and Vail's default start is just above it. A resort without a start landmark can have a placed start in the same file (FEAT-219): Breckenridge starts at the top of Imperial Bowl, by its menu shot. Either one beats the pack's `meta.start`.
- Lifts: towers, cables and moving chairs from OSM. E at a bottom terminal boards (prompt card, floating markers and a loading zone show where); while riding, E skips to the top and holding W fast-forwards. The rider sits on a real chair. Chairlift bases have lift-line mazes (2 to 4 lanes by capacity, wait and load lines, funnel fences, a name board) whose ropes are colliders; with a maze built, boarding works only inside the corral. Settings > Map and world turns the mazes off.
- Time trials (Settings > Gameplay): start and finish gates on real runs, splits, a ghost of your best run, boundary walls with a 5 s grace, an arrow to the finish. T jumps to the nearest start.
- Loading screen (BUG-063, `ui/Loading.ts`, `warmUp` in `main.ts`): after the menu, a DOM overlay paints first, then the session builds behind it. Warm-up streams terrain, trees and rocks around the start with a 12 ms per-frame build budget and no rendering, until no tile is downloading and no chunk or tree cell is waiting (20 s cap), then `renderer.compileAsync` compiles the shaders, and the overlay stays up for the first 3 real frames. While riding, chunk builds stop after 4 ms a frame (`Terrain.budgetMs`, max 10) and tree cell builds after 3 ms (`Vegetation.budgetMs`); built parent chunks cover any gaps. Headless runs skip the screen and keep the fixed counts, so scripted runs stay deterministic.
- Outrun (Esc > Mode > Outrun, FEAT-097 to FEAT-101; design in `docs/research/outrun-mode.md`): a snow monster chases you down the mountain after a head start. It follows your breadcrumbs by arc length (no pathfinding, any pack), charges straight with line of sight inside 60 m, rubber-bands on your recent speed so crashes and stops feed it, and rides the cable tower to tower when you take a lift (no lift skip while it runs). Difficulties Chill / Normal / SkiFree, escape by reaching the base (lowest building cluster near a lift bottom) or Endless (a second one at 3 min). F burns adrenaline (close calls, clean landings, fast carving) for a 4 s boost, hold G to look back. Spatial sound (HRTF gallop, roars, breathing, heartbeat), music ducks, muffles and cuts as it closes in with war drums from the jukebox. Caught: slow-mo grab, rip in half (snow and confetti, no gore), unhinged jaw, two bites, the board spat out, a burp, then the EATEN card with best scores; Gentle swallows you whole. `node tools/outruncheck.mjs vail shots-outrun --gpu` runs the whole flow headless.
- Replay and rewind (FEAT-043): the session is recorded. K opens the replay viewer (play, scrub, speed, follow / trackside / free camera, FOV, time of day, hide HUD, P photo); holding Backspace rewinds along the recording and letting go carries on from the last good ground (it cancels a time trial).
- Sky and weather: real sun position for the resort's lon/lat and date (resort default, real time, or a fixed hour and month); volumetric clouds from bluebird to broken, plus thin high cloud (streaky cirrus and faint contrails); a snowstorm option.
- Cameras: chase (behind and above, looking over the rider's head at the snow ahead), close (tight and low), far (angled broadcast view looking down at the rider), cinematic (low, off to one side, long lens), first person, helmet cam (wide and level, on top of the helmet) and free fly (backtick). Hold Alt or the right mouse button to look around, hold G to look behind in any camera (FEAT-124). Speed feel: FOV widens with speed, the camera rumbles with the snow under the board (none on corduroy, more off-piste) and shakes on landings, and speed lines streak past; each has a switch in Settings. First person sees through goggles (dark frame, edge tint in the lens colour, snow drift, frost in powder, darken and shake on hard landings) with your legs, board and arms in view. Goggle lenses (FEAT-130, `world/Lens.ts`): in first person only, the scene renders to a half-float target exactly as it would to the screen (tone mapped and sRGB encoded, so haze and particles blend the same; the target sets `isXRRenderTarget`, an undocumented three 0.172 internal, to get that, so recheck it on a three upgrade: `lenscheck.mjs` fails if the near-neutral pass stops matching no lens), then one full-screen pass applies the lens transmission, a two-band local contrast boost on the lens-weighted luminance (blurs from the target's mip chain, faded out at hard edges so trees do not halo) and the lens brightness as a plain gain (mirrors dim; a soft roll-off above 0.85 keeps the warm tints from clipping). Off and Clear skip the pass, as do third person and the helmet cam. Auto picks by weather and sun (`autoLens`); the rider's goggle material (`RiderView.setLens`) and the frame tint follow the lens worn. See-through trees (FEAT-155, `world/TreeFade.ts`, Settings > Camera, on by default): only a tree that hides the rider or the line ahead fades; a tree off to the side with open snow behind it stays solid. The line runs from the rider to a look-ahead point 10 m out along the travel direction (smoothed over 0.6 s so linked turns do not sweep it; where the camera looks below 1.5 m/s), plus 1 m per m/s up to 30 m, eased over 0.4 s, and a second point eased over 1.5 s keeps the old line covered for a moment after a turn. The tree shaders (the LOD0 and LOD1 materials of the tree kit, `world/trees/materials.ts`; LOD2 and the impostors start at 400 m, past any line the fade covers) take each tree's trunk-to-top line and the line ahead into the camera's view plane from shared uniforms (`treeFadeUniforms`, no per-tree CPU work): a tree fades when its silhouette (0.8 of the crown radius) comes within 0.75 m of the line there (eased over 0.75 m), and the near side of its crown is nearer the camera than the stretch of line it covers (full by 2 m nearer). So a crown hanging between the camera and the rider fades, and a tree just past the end of the look-ahead does not. A faded tree discards up to 75% of its fragments in an 8x8 screen-space Bayer pattern, so there is no sorting, depth write stays on and the shadow pass draws the tree whole. The lower trunk keeps 30% more, and a trunk within 1 m of the board's path over the next 0.5 s stays solid, easing back to fadeable by 3 m. The fade ramps in and out over 0.3 s and is off in first person, the helmet cam, free fly and the eat.
- Run stats (FEAT-055): each run tracks time, top speed, distance, vertical, longest air, biggest drop, max lateral g, the named runs ridden (nearest mapped run line within 25 m) and the line, sampled every 2 s. A run starts at 3 m/s on the board and ends on boarding a lift, a 4 s stop or a restart; under 60 m of vertical it is dropped. The after-run card shows the summary; the HUD shows the live run. Day totals (vertical, runs, lifts) and per-run personal bests (fastest time, top speed, keyed by the first named run) persist in localStorage under `session-day` and `run-pbs`. Paused during Outrun; no card in headless runs.
- Maps: minimap with nearby lift names, and a full map (M) with zoom, pan and click to drop in anywhere; landmarks show as gold diamonds.
- Sound: layered surface-aware board sound (groomed, powder, hardpack, crust), rock scrapes, carve and wind layers, varied one-shots, lift sounds.

Ghosts (`world/AttractGhosts.ts`): takes listed under the resort in `game/public/attract/index.json`, each an `AttractTake` (version, pack, discipline, stance, an outfit preset name and the `ReplaySample` slice from 0); each drives its own `Rider` (no physics: `Replay.overlay`) and `RiderView`; all start from their beginning together when the menu goes live, and each starts over as soon as it ends (FEAT-179). A take marked `backdrop: true` was made from lines drawn on the shot and ridden by the rider physics with a steering bot (the partners are the lead's take shifted to the side and back): `tools/ghost_lines.mjs` makes them (see Test tools, "Ghost riders from lines drawn on the menu"), names them `<shot>-<line>-<n>.json` and lists them per shot under `shotGhosts` in `index.json` (also in `ghosts`), with the shot and the pack's `meta.build` in each take; a new shot replaces the old one and its generated takes. - Controllers (FEAT-137): `sim/Pad.ts` is the one source every reader asks (`activePad()`): the Gamepad API on the standard mapping, a Sony pad with no mapping remapped from the raw DirectInput order (Firefox; best effort), or the DualSense over WebHID while it is live (then the Gamepad API copy is skipped so presses don't double up). It detects the kind from the id (Sony vendor `054c`, DualSense `0ce6`, Edge `0df2`, "DualSense" / "Wireless Controller"; Xbox `045e`), holds the button names and `DEFAULT_PAD` (action to two standard button indices; the sticks and the pause buttons Options / PS are fixed). `Controls` feeds the buttons through `held()` / `action()` like the keys (`padKeys`, triggers held past `padCfg.trigDead`), keeps `padOnce` / `padHeld` for the map and the replay viewer, and `padMute()` ignores buttons held through a menu press until they are let go. `ui/PadGlyphs.ts` draws the buttons (our own SVG shapes for the PlayStation face buttons, lettered circles for Xbox, pills for the rest); from `BUMP_A0` m/s2, full `BUMP_A1` above it, times speed up to 8 m/s, none where the mask is more than 0.7 groomed (Vail's elevation model undulates under its groomers: up to a quarter strength straight down Whiskey Jack before); bumps, chop, rock and soft touchdowns under a `landingShock` of about 0.4 are scaled by `RIDE_RUMBLE` (0.35) so crashes, real landings and a press stand out), a held press (a hum, then a beat that quickens toward either end of `pressBalance`: the strong motor toward the slam, the weak one toward dropping flat), trial events and Outrun footfalls into rumble (`vibrationActuator.playEffect('dual-rumble')` renewed every 80 ms, or the HID motors) and drives the lightbar and triggers. Chrome on Windows exposes no `vibrationActuator` for a DualSense, so `PadNav` shows the menus' `.ds-connect` offer (a DualSense on the Gamepad API, WebHID available, not connected; the picker needs a trusted mouse click, a Cross press only explains that), `padFx.test()` backs Settings > Test vibration and `padFx.rumbleStatus` the `?debug=pad` line (with the motors now and `padFx.why`, the share from each source: bumps, chop, rock, landing, crash, press, trial, monster). `sim/DualSense.ts` is the WebHID driver (the USB output report is the 47-byte common block, sized from the device's descriptor when it gives one: the browser refuses any other size; write errors go to `sendError` and the status): report layouts from the Linux `hid-playstation` driver (USB 0x01 / 0x02, Bluetooth 0x31 with the CRC-32 seeded by 0xA2, feature report 0x05 to get full Bluetooth reports, 0x20 for the firmware's rumble style), trigger effects (Feedback 0x21, Weapon 0x25) from Nielk1's notes; the gyro sign and scale (about 16 units per deg/s) feed `Controls.gyroDx / gyroDy`, a touchpad swipe of 550 / 1920 within 0.6 s cycles the camera.

### States

The session is a state machine (`core/StateMachine.ts`, FEAT-162). One state is active; it decides which systems tick and it takes the input, so a menu and the riding keys never both see a key.

| state | entered | who moves the rider | keys it takes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `loading` | the session starts | nobody (no frame runs: the loop starts when it ends) | none |
| `attract` (main menu) | after loading with a menu; Quit to main menu | nobody, hidden (`menu`) | none: input is suspended, the menu has it |
| `riding` | RIDE; leaving any state below | the physics (`ride`) | every riding action; Esc pauses |
| `rewind` | the rewind key held while riding | the recording (`recording`) | the rewind key; Esc lands where it got to, then pauses |
| `paused` | Esc while riding | nothing ticks; the frame only renders | Esc resumes; the pause menu's own actions |
| `replay` (K) | K, or the pause menu's Replay | the recording (`recording`) | the viewer's keys; Esc, K or Circle close it |
| `map` (M) | the full map opening, however (M, the pause menu, RIDE's "or drop in") | nobody, though a lift carries on (`hold`) | the map's (mouse, touch, stick, + / -); Esc, M or Circle close it; the music key |

A state that changes the state inside its update hands the rest of that frame to the new state (its update runs next with the same dt), so Esc closing the map or K opening the viewer acts in the frame the key went down. The Map state follows `ResortMap.onOpenChange`, so a drop-in click, a tool calling `__sim.map.close()` or the pause menu all move it. While Outrun's monster has the rider (`caught`) the eat sequence moves it, whatever the state asked. `__sim.state` is the active state's name; `__sim.attract` is still there.

### The frame, in order

`Session.ts` `tickFrame(dt)` reads the devices (`RideFrame.readInput`: `controls.update`, the right stick and gyro into the mouse look), then runs the active state's update and render. Paused renders and closes the input frame. Every other state reads its own keys, then runs `RideFrame.update` and `draw`, which do these in this order every drawn frame (FEAT-160, FEAT-162). Keep the order when moving code: the view steps draw the pose the sim steps just made, the cameras read the rider view (the eye), and streaming and the sky follow the final camera.

1. The state's keys: riding's restart hold, trial key (`modes.trialKey`), replay and rewind (they switch state), camera cycle, map, piste, track, fly, strap in or out, boost (`modes.boostKey`); the replay viewer's keys and the replayed pose; the rewind's scrub and pose; the map's stick, zoom and drop-in; behind the main menu the input is suspended and the backdrop camera drifts.
2. Adaptive quality takes the frame time (FEAT-062); the touch buttons shown or hidden; the eye camera's photo look (no rider, spray, markers or piste lines).
3. Simulation on the sim pose, as far as the state's drive allows (`RUNS` in `RideFrame.ts`): lift ride, then the rider (its own 1/120 s fixed steps), replay recording, run stats, rumble. The lift ride, Outrun (step 5) and the monsters step on the frame `dt`, not the fixed clock: moving them onto it would change their results (FEAT-166).
4. View sync on the render pose (interpolated between the last two physics steps): rider view, snow off the trees the rider brushes (FEAT-217), tracks, prints, puffs. Then the sim pose comes back.
5. Modes (`modes.update`, FEAT-164), each one's update then its HUD, in this order: Free ride (nothing); Outrun: its sim, its events emitted on the bus (sound, rumble, camera kick, toast and the eat start subscribe), the countdown beeps, the eat sequence, the monsters, the Outrun HUD; Time trials: the sim, gates, ghost, route walls, the trial HUD.
6. Audio (reads the sim pose and the lift).
7. Cameras on the render pose: free, eye, first person, replay trackside, else the chase camera; then `modes.afterCamera`: the eat sequence overrides it and the chase sounds take it as their listener; lens, goggles, speed lines. The press meter projects through the final camera.
8. Sky and effects from the camera: shadows (CSM), clouds, snowfall, particle light, tree fade (FEAT-155), the deformation stamp (FEAT-044).
9. Streaming around the rider (or the camera when detached): terrain, prefetch, trees, rocks; snow cover, the skied-out day, moguls; roads, the chair, lifts, bridges, lift lines, landmarks; the live sun.
10. Prompt and key hints, the sky's follow, tile eviction, the map (the full map while it is open, else the minimap), horizon labels.
11. Render, then the P-key shot (the drawing buffer is only readable in the same task), `controls.endFrame`, and the replay overlay restored.
12. Frame rate and the HUD line.

### Inside the rider step and the rider view

Each rider step (`Rider.update`, in step 3 above, FEAT-165): the squat and press lean ease, the press balance (`Press.ts`), then the surface and snow under the rider, then one of strapping in or out, walking (`OnFoot.ts`), the crash slide, the air (`Jumps.ts` `airStep`, landing in `Tricks.ts`), or on the snow (`Ride.ts` `rideStep`): gravity along the slope, the edge and carve (`EdgeModel.ts` `carve`, which ends the step on a washout), the brake (`Brake.ts`), the sideways grip and edge catch (`EdgeModel.ts` `grip`), the drag, the push and glide, the jump, following the ground, the collisions.

The rider view (`RiderView.update`, in step 4 above): on foot it hands over to `FootPose.ts`; riding, the orientation and the set-down into powder (`Effects.ts`), the inclination (`BodyPose.ts` `incline`), the board's roll and flex (`BoardFlex.ts`), the ragdoll if crashed, the pose blend and physical layer (`BodyPose.ts`), FK, legs, head and hands (`Limbs.ts` `settleBody`), the spray.

### Events

Cross-system news goes through `core/Events.ts` (FEAT-163): `events.on(type, fn)` (returns its `off`), `events.off`, `events.emit({ type, ... })`, with every event in the one `GameEvent` union. `emit` calls the listeners at once, in the order they subscribed, so a listener runs inside the frame step that emitted (the list above still holds). The sim classes keep their own hooks (`rider.onTrick`, `trial.onEvent`, ...) because the node checks drive them without a bus; `session/eventSources.ts` points those hooks at `emit`, the frame emits the landmarks it pulls and `OutrunMode` the Outrun events.

| event | emitted by | listeners |
|---|---|---|
| `trick`, `washout`, `pressEnd` | `Rider` | toast |
| `footstep` | `Rider` (skate push), `RiderView` (foot plant) | audio |
| `footPlant` | `RiderView` | boot prints |
| `trial` (armed, start, split, finish, cancel) | `TimeTrial` | trial panel, then chime, then rumble |
| `runEnd` | `RunStats` | run card |
| `landmark` | the frame (`landmarks.maintain`) | toast |
| `song` | `GameAudio` | toast |
| `outrun` | `OutrunMode.update` (`outrun.events`) | toast (second monster), audio, rumble, then `OutrunMode.listen`: camera kick (footfalls), eat start (caught, after the audio) |
| `eatSound`, `treeCrack` | `Eat`, `Monsters` | audio (the chase sounds) |

A new event: add it to the union, emit it where the news is known, subscribe where it is used (`ui/HudEvents.ts`, `GameAudio.listen`, `PadFeedback.listen`, the session for the world, or the mode's own `listen`). Landings, crashes and lift boarding are not events yet: audio, rumble, the camera and the spray each read the rider's state in their own update, on different rules, so one event would change when they fire.

`.github/workflows/checks.yml` (FEAT-172) runs the checks on every PR (opened, pushed to, reopened) and on pushes to `main`, as separate jobs, so each shows as its own check on the PR. The header of the workflow file is the canonical list.

Then `game/tools/attract_look.mjs --check` (FEAT-177) warns, as an annotation on `game/public/attract/index.json`, when the PR changes a file that affects the main menu still but the still's look key no longer matches: re-render it with `shoot_attract.mjs` on Drew's GPU (CI has neither the GPU nor the Vail pack). The synthetic pack is built once by the `synth-pack` job and cached on a hash of the pipeline's Python (`tools/ci/synth_key.sh`), so it is rebuilt only when the pipeline changes; when a PR changes it, the stills' "before" uses `main`'s own synthetic pack. The counts are exact run to run. Stills differ a little where the chase camera settles (a percent or two on `01_spawn_chase`); the other views are exact. The stills are the synthetic mountain at `lowgfx` on a software renderer, so they are a smoke test, not a look check: judge visual changes on Vail on the GPU.

**Stays local** (needs a real pack or a real GPU, or is too slow on software rendering): the real-pack checks (`lenscheck`, `outruncheck`, `snowcheck`, `treefadecheck`, `shoot_spots`, `aerial`, `views`, `treeperf`), stills on Vail or Breckenridge, `perf-check` frame times, `--headed` runs, pack builds from raw data, and the browser checks that draw every frame (`powdercheck`, `menucheck`, `leadcheck`, `trailcheck`, `statecheck`, `treeshot`: minutes each on a runner's software renderer, `BROWSER_LOCAL` in the workflow). Run what the change needs on Drew's PC with `--gpu` and say so in the PR.

**On every push, from a hook** (FEAT-183, Drew's PC only): switch it on once per clone with `git config core.hooksPath .githooks` and `git config sa.gpuBudgets true` (Drew's repo has no `extensions.worktreeConfig`, so both reach every worktree). Then a plain `git push` from a PR's worktree runs the check described below on the GPU before the push, when all of these hold: the push goes to `origin`, it carries the worktree's `HEAD` to a branch with an open PR, the PR's diff against its merge base with `origin/main` touches `game/`, `pipeline/`, `tools/ci/` or `checks.yml` (CI's `changes` filter, so a docs-only push goes straight through), and the PR has no `stills` label. On a pass, a detached process (`tools/ci/render_budgets_post.mjs`) waits until GitHub has the commit, then sets the status and the comment; CI's job reads the status about 30 seconds after the push. The hook never blocks a push: a fail, a refusal (a dirty tree, `node_modules` not matching the lockfile, a `gh` login other than the owner) or an error prints one `render-budgets:` line, sets nothing, and CI runs the check as usual. If the status lands after CI's job has looked, CI runs its own check too (only runner time is lost; re-runs of that commit then skip). Skip it for a quick push with `git push --no-verify` or `SA_SKIP_GPU_BUDGETS=1 git push`. Logs and outputs: `~/.cache/snowboardanywhere-ci/runs/<sha>/` (`hook.log`, `post.log`). The first push of a new branch has no PR yet, so CI runs that one. `core.hooksPath` hides `.git/hooks`: `.githooks/pre-push` runs a local `.git/hooks/pre-push` first, but other local hooks stop running. That includes Drew's local `post-merge` that kept `spotify-private` in step with `main`: once the hook path is set, merge `main` into `spotify-private` by hand (see `CLAUDE.md`) and resolve conflicts there. The shared code is `tools/ci/render_budgets_lib.mjs`.

**Run render-budgets locally** (FEAT-176, Drew's PC only): `node tools/ci/local_render_budgets.mjs --push` in the PR's worktree, with everything committed. It builds the merge base with `origin/main` and `HEAD` in their own detached worktrees, runs `HEAD`'s `cishot.mjs --gpu` on both with the synthetic pack CI would build (keyed by `tools/ci/synth_key.sh` on an LF copy of `pipeline/`, built once into `~/.cache/snowboardanywhere-ci`), then `render_report.py`: about 2 minutes, most of it the two Vite builds. On a pass it pushes `HEAD` to the PR's branch, sets the commit status `render-budgets/local` on it (`passed base=<merge base> tools=<hash> synth=<key>`) and writes the report into the PR's `<!-- ci-stills -->` comment, marked as run locally with the machine's name and the GPU's. On a fail it prints the report, pushes nothing and posts nothing. Without `--push`, `HEAD` must already be the PR's head on GitHub; that only helps re-runs, because CI's job starts about 30 seconds after a push and has its own result by the time a local run finishes. It refuses on a dirty tree, a PR with the `stills` label, `node_modules` that don't match the lockfile, or a `gh` login other than the repo owner. The PR must exist (its first push runs the check in CI as usual). Options: `--pr N`, `--keep` (keep the build worktrees), `--cache DIR`.

CI's `render-budgets` job then checks the head commit's statuses first and skips the rest of the job (it still reports success, and `render-budgets-comment` adds "skipped in CI: passed locally at <sha>" to the comment) only when all of these hold: a `render-budgets/local` success on that exact SHA, set by the repo owner; its base is the PR's current merge base with `main`; its tools hash matches `tools/ci/render_budgets_key.sh` in the commit CI checked out (git blob ids of `cishot.mjs`, its browser and path helpers, `render_report.py`, `synth_key.sh`, the runner, `checks.yml` and `game/package-lock.json`); its synth key matches CI's; and no stills were asked for. A new push, a rebase onto a newer `main`, a PR that changes any of those tools (or `main` changing them since the PR branched), a `stills` label or a status from anyone else runs it in CI. When this was added, the counts on Drew's RTX 2070 SUPER (ANGLE D3D11) matched a runner's SwiftShader run exactly in every view (PR #416 has the numbers). Absolute counts still move a little between machines and runs (another runner counted one more texture on the same code), as does the heap, so the check never compares numbers across machines: before and after always come from the same run on the same machine, locally as in CI. Cloud sessions don't run it: no GPU, and CI covers them.

**The review reads it.** `pr-review.yml` waits (up to 40 minutes) for the Checks run on the PR's head commit and writes `checks-summary.md` (each job's result, the end of each failed job's log, the compile and budgets comment); the reviewer (`.claude/agents/pr-reviewer.md`, "CI checks") treats a failed check as blocking.

**Rerun:** the PR's Checks tab > Re-run jobs, or Actions > Checks > Run workflow with the PR number (that also refreshes the PR comment; tick `stills` for the before / after stills). `pr-feedback.yml` starts it that way after each push, because a push with the workflow's own token starts no `pull_request` run.

**Add a check:** a Node-only tool that exits 1 on failure goes in `PHYSICS_CHECKS`; a browser tool that passes on `synth` with `--cpu` in a few minutes goes in `BROWSER_CHECKS`; anything else is a new job or a step in one. Keep the tool runnable locally with the same command.

**Required checks** (optional): to block merging on a red check, Settings > Branches > a rule for `main` > Require status checks > pick `typecheck-build` and `pipeline-tests` (they only appear after their first run). Drew switches this on himself.

## README update (`readme-update.yml`)

## Pipeline scripts (`pipeline/`)

Flow: `resorts/<slug>.json` -> `gen_manifest.py` -> `manifests/<slug>.json` -> `fetch.ps1` -> `raw/<slug>/` -> `build_pack.py` -> `packs/<slug>/`.

- `resorts/<slug>.json` resort config (origin, UTM bbox and zone, rings, NAIP items, lidar nodes). Optional keys: `lidar.extra`, more EPT datasets for the parts the main one misses, each with its own `nodes_file` and an optional `utm_bbox` clip (Big Sky's east strip; they download to `raw/<slug>/ept/<dataset>/`); `exclude`, members-only areas whose runs and lifts the build drops (`{"name", "osm_way": <id>}` for a polygon in `osm/ski.json`, or `{"name", "lonlat": [[lon, lat], ...]}`; ways tagged `access=private` go anyway, `osm_filter.py`); `treeline_m`, to override the measured treeline. Resorts so far: `vail`, `breckenridge`, `bigsky` (DATA-021, UTM 12N).
- `gen_manifest.py` writes `manifests/<slug>.json`, the download list (needs `pip install pyproj`).
- `fetch.ps1` resumable Windows downloader (about 1.4 GB for Vail; `-RawDir` picks the destination), launched by `fetch_<slug>.cmd` in the repo root (`build_<slug>.cmd` builds the pack after it, for Breckenridge and Big Sky). Run one fetch at a time.
- `fetch_static.ps1` (launcher `fetch_static.cmd`) Geofabrik Colorado OSM extract and USGS GNIS names into `raw/static/`.
- `fetch_landfire.mjs <slug> <email> [EVT|EVC|EVH] [--raw <raw_root>] [--force]` (launcher `fetch_landfire.cmd [slug] [raw_root]`, which fetches all three and asks for the slug when none is given) LANDFIRE clips via the LFPS job service: vegetation type (EVT) into `raw/<slug>/landfire/`, cover (EVC) and height (EVH, DATA-010) into `landfire/evc/` and `landfire/evh/`. A product already on disk is skipped unless `--force`.
- `fetch_grooming.py <slug> <raw_dir>` the resort's real grooming history (Vail's nightly reports, from Internet Archive snapshots of its terrain status page) into `raw/<slug>/grooming/`. Groomed runs in the pack come only from these reports and OSM `piste:grooming` tags.
- `extract_osm.py` cuts `osm/*.json` and `names/gnis.json` out of the static files (needs `pip install osmium`), the Overpass-free route for future resorts.
- `build_pack.py <slug> <raw_dir> <out_dir>` raw inputs to a resort pack (`pip install -r pipeline/requirements.txt`), about 4 minutes and 234 MB for Vail.
- `publish_pack.py <pack_dir> [--status preview|playable] [--tier gold|silver|bronze] [--report <md>] [--dry-run]` (FEAT-222) publishes a built pack to the resorts repo, [MapPacker-SnowboardAnywhere](https://github.com/drewkeriazes/MapPacker-SnowboardAnywhere) (cloned next to this checkout the first time; `gh` signed in with push access). Refuses a pack whose `meta.json` `formatVersion` is not `packwriter.FORMAT_VERSION` and a zip over 1.9 GiB. Zips the pack stored with fixed timestamps (the same pack gives the same sha256; `debug/` and dot files left out), creates release `<slug>-v<N>` (one past the highest release or catalog version) with the zip, `meta.json` and the report, then updates `catalog.json`, copies the resort's config, EPT node lists and manifest into the clone and commits and pushes. `status` comes from the pack's `report.json` (`pack_report.py`, below) when it was made for the pack's current `build`: `playable` when the report passes, `preview` otherwise or without one, and `--status playable` is refused unless the report passes (EPIC-15 rule 2). `--dry-run` zips and prints the catalog entry only. The game side is `game/tools/get_pack.mjs`.
- `pack_report.py <pack_dir> [--raw <raw_dir>] [--config <json>] [--against <report.json>] [--gate]` (FEAT-175) the pack's scorecard on the DATA-022 new-resort checklist (#439), about 15 s for Vail. Writes `report.json` and `report.md` into the pack (or `--out`) and prints the markdown: one line per checklist item (F for the pack format, 1 to 14 and 8b MVP, 15 to 20 polish) as pass, warn, fail or skip, then the numbers behind them against Vail's (`REFERENCE`, or `--against` another report): rock, canopy and groomed shares, canopy left on run centre lines, crowns + stems per km² over the bounds and per 100 m band, crowns on lift lines, trees per hectare within 100 m of each compare spot, runs whose steepest 30 m pitch disagrees with their OSM difficulty, and whether the start is on snow, on a run and clear of trees. `--raw` checks the raw files behind lines 2 to 4 and 16; without it those lines are judged from the pack (LANDFIRE, line 4, is then a skip unless the attribution credits it). The pack is playable when no MVP line is fail or skip; `--gate` exits 1 otherwise, and `publish_pack.py` reads the result. Run it after every build and paste `report.md` into the PR.
- `synth_pack.py <out_dir>` synthetic test mountain in the same format, for client work without real data.
- `packwriter.py` shared pack writer (Terrarium height tiles, masks) used by `build_pack.py` and `synth_pack.py`; every `meta.json` write stamps `build` and `pipeline` (the commit of the pipeline that wrote it, `+dirty` with uncommitted changes in `pipeline/`). `FORMAT_VERSION` (FEAT-222) is the pack format both builds write to `meta.json`; it must equal `PACK_FORMAT` in `game/src/pack/format.ts` (`tests/test_publish_pack.py` checks), and both are bumped on any pack-format change (`docs/pack-format.md` > formatVersion).
- `stands.py` (FEAT-074) stand density per 4 m cell: the stem chance from canopy, height and species less the detected crowns, and the `stands/` images (`docs/pack-format.md` > Stands), used by `build_pack.py`, `synth_pack.py` and `cover.py`.
- `terrain_ao.py` baked sky visibility (terrain ambient occlusion) from the DEM, used by the pack build.
- `piste_dir.py` piste direction (DATA-002): the groomed runs' axis per 2 m cell as the `dir/` tile set, used by both pack builds; `python pipeline/piste_dir.py packs/<slug>` adds it to an existing pack from `features.json` and the mask tiles without a rebuild. The terrain (`Terrain.ts`) points it downhill and feeds it to the corduroy as the `fall` attribute, falling back to the smoothed fall line where a pack has no set.
- `cover.py <slug> <raw_dir> <pack_dir>` (FEAT-153) forest cover levels for the terrain at a distance (`cover/*.png`, see `docs/pack-format.md`): the pack's own canopy at 4 m with its OSM runs burned in and weighted by the drawn trees (BUG-069: R by their crown share for the view from far off, and a `stands` image with only the closed stands at least 40 m across for the near view, so sparse stands and small clumps stay snow with their trees; rerun it on a pack built before that), and (DATA-019) the mid (16 m, 40 km) and far (100 m, 200 km) levels from ESA WorldCover and the ETH canopy height with the pistes of every resort in view burned in. The pack level is built first and the mid and far forest is calibrated to it by WorldCover share and aspect (BUG-081), so the share does not jump at the pack edge; inside the far tree ring the mid and far forest comes from its lidar crowns instead (run `far_trees.py` first); rerun it on a built pack to get either. Runs as the last step of `build_pack.py`, or standalone on a built pack in seconds.
- `species.py` (DATA-020) tree species ids 0 to 7 (spruce, aspen, krummholz, fir, lodgepole, Douglas-fir, limber pine, snag) from the LANDFIRE class, elevation, aspect, distance to OSM streams, exposed crests and grey dead crowns, and the shrub set (willow, juniper, sagebrush, young conifer, Gambel oak); `build_pack.py` stage 8 calls it, logs per-species counts per 200 m elevation band and writes `debug/species.png` into the pack. Without a LANDFIRE clip it falls back to elevation and aspect. LANDFIRE maps most of a ski area as `Urban Evergreen Forest` or `Developed-*`, which name no species (DATA-015): `natural_evt` gives each such pixel a natural forest class drawn from the stands within about 300 m, and NAIP breaks the tie there. Aspen is canopy brighter in visible and NIR than conifer, and lodgepole is canopy lower in NDVI than spruce-fir, both averaged over about 8 m. The thresholds are fitted per pack on its own natural LANDFIRE classes (`fit_aspen`, `fit_lodgepole`), so each resort's imagery calibrates itself. A fit that doesn't separate the two classes is not used: aspen then takes Vail's thresholds, and lodgepole gets no tie-breaker (Big Sky's NAIP has no lodgepole NDVI split). A pack without LANDFIRE uses Vail's aspen thresholds. The build log prints the fitted thresholds, their hit and false rates, and the class mix the built pixels were filled with. The client draws every id with its own species from the tree kit (FEAT-156); shrubs are not drawn yet (FEAT-159).
- `moguls.py` (FEAT-059) scores each run for mogul fields (grooming reports, difficulty, slope and aspect along the centre line, lift-top traffic; cat tracks, glades and greens never) and paints the amplitude raster the pack build writes as the `moguls` tile set. The build log lists the top-scoring runs. Tests: `cd pipeline && python -m pytest tests`.
- `ept_select.py <slug> [--dry]` picks the EPT lidar nodes over a resort for the manifest from its config: every node with points in `utm_bbox`, down to `lidar.max_depth_resort` where it touches the ski area (`lonlat_bbox`) and `max_depth_outside` elsewhere, for the main dataset and each of `lidar.extra`; writes each source's `nodes_file` (not with `--dry`). With `lidar.far` (`pad_m`, `max_depth`, `nodes_file`; BUG-081) it also picks the far tree ring's nodes (the box grown by `pad_m`, down to `max_depth`, less the main list), which `gen_manifest.py` sends to `raw/<slug>/ept_far/`.
- `far_trees.py <slug> <raw_dir> <pack_dir>` (BUG-081) the far tree ring: the lidar crowns on the ridges round the resort, outside the pack bounds, as `farTrees/` (`docs/pack-format.md` > Far tree ring). Runs inside `build_pack.py` before the cover when the config has `lidar.far` and the nodes are fetched, or standalone on a built pack (about 2 minutes and 3 GB for Vail's 5 km ring), followed by `cover.py`.
- `canopy_spots.py <pack> [--before <pack>] [--radius 80]` (BUG-028) canopy cover round the photo-pair spots in `docs/compare/<slug>/`, from the mask alpha: over the whole disc and over its off-run part (non-glade run corridors, roads and buildings left out, with the run widths `build_pack.py` cuts the forest with, `RUN_HALF_WIDTH`). Checks the BUG-028 targets (off-run canopy at least 0.5 within 80 m of Born Free and 100 m of Mid-Vail; with `--before`, the open bowls gain no more than 0.02) and exits 1 if one fails. Where the pack has tree instances it also lists the live detected crowns (snags left out) per hectare of the disc's off-run ground (DATA-011). Run it after a rebuild that touches the canopy rules or the crown detection.
- `treeline.py` (DATA-021) the resort's treeline from its own canopy by elevation (where the canopy share falls under half its peak), written to `meta.json` as `treeline`; the build also shifts the species' elevation bands (tuned on Vail's 3,480 m) by the difference, and the client puts its procedural treeline, open valley floor and snow-ghost band there (`game/src/pack/treeline.ts`). No value where the box never leaves the forest (Vail), which keeps Colorado's.
- `osm_filter.py` (DATA-021) drops private runs and lifts (the config's `exclude` areas and `access=private` tags) from `osm/ski.json` before the build reads it.
- `prep_textures.py [work_dir]` fetches the CC0 ambientCG rock and talus sets and writes the game's surface textures into `game/public/textures/`.

The data hosts (USGS, Planetary Computer, Overpass, Mapterhorn) are not reachable from cloud sandboxes; fetching runs on Drew's PC. Before touching raw data read [data/vail-raw-data-inventory.md](data/vail-raw-data-inventory.md). Pack layout: [pack-format.md](pack-format.md).

## URL parameters

Parsed once in `game/src/core/params.ts` (FEAT-160); a new parameter goes there and in this list. The spawn (`x`, `y`, `h`) is read in `spawn.ts` when a ride starts, because the map and the menu rewrite it; `debug=pad` is read in `ui/PadNav.ts`.

- `pack=<slug>` resort pack to load (`vail`, `breckenridge`, `bigsky`, `synth`)
- `x=&y=&h=` spawn in local metres with heading in degrees
- `cam=fp` start in first person
- `cam=eye&pitch=&fov=&eye=&spot=` still camera at eye height (default 1.7 m) over `x, y`, looking along `h` with `pitch` degrees up (negative is down) and `fov` vertical degrees; HUD and rider hidden, P saves the shot (into `docs/compare/` when `spot=<slug>/<view>` is set). With `debug` too, Shift+P makes the view the resort's main menu shot (FEAT-181: a form for its name, place and region, then `/__attract/shot` writes `game/public/attract/<pack>/shots.json`). Make these links from a Street View URL with `python tools/gmaps2game.py`.
- `time=now` or `time=16.5` override the time of day
- `weather=clear|cirrus|few|scattered|broken|overcast|flurries|storm` override the cloud cover; `valleyfog` forces valley fog on
- `rider=ski` or `rider=board`
- `menuframe` (FEAT-177) keeps the live frame at the main menu's crossfade in `window.__menuFrame` (a PNG data URL) and whether a ghost was in frame in `window.__menuFrameGhosts` (`shoot_attract.mjs --compare`)
- `stance=regular` or `stance=goofy` (FEAT-139, overrides Settings > Gameplay > Stance)
- `debug=pad` (FEAT-137, also in the `npm run host` build) an overlay of every connected controller: id, mapping, buttons held with their values, axes, whether it can rumble, the WebHID DualSense's transport, report count, battery, gyro and touch, and what the game reads
- `lens=auto|rose|orange|yellow|green|blue|purple|clear|off` (FEAT-130, overrides Settings > Camera > Goggle lens)
- `debug` (not in the `npm run host` build) adds a HUD line with the edge angle, lateral acceleration, turn radius and turn rate, the heading's angle off the fall line with the slope and the edge catch's chance per second (FEAT-134), a line with the snow condition weights (FEAT-053), sliders for the body lead / board lag chain (BUG-056), the replay viewer's I / O / L keys that record a take for the main menu's ghosts (FEAT-128; see Test tools, "Recording the main menu's ghosts") and the eye camera's Shift+P that saves the view as the resort's menu shot (FEAT-181)
- `nomoguls` flattens the mogul fields (FEAT-059); with `debug`, the resort map (M) under-strokes runs scoring 0.3 or more in orange and the hover line shows the nearest run's score. `debug=moguls` (BUG-062) tints the terrain by mogul amplitude: green where the bumps are in the chunk geometry (1 and 2 m chunks), red where the shader alone draws them (4 to 16 m), faint magenta on chunks too coarse for either
- `snowdepth=<m>` (FEAT-159) one snowpack depth everywhere for burying the shrubs, instead of the season's (`snowdepth=0` shows every shrub at full height, `snowdepth=1.5` a deep year)
- `lowgfx` reduced graphics for software GPUs; `headless` is used by the test tools; `csm` / `nocsm` force cascaded shadows on or off; `noimpostors` disables the tree impostors (LOD2 crossed quads run out to the far edge instead); `nodeform` turns the snow deformation off (FEAT-044, for A/B shots); `bridgeguess` adds the segment-chord bridge detector

## Milestones

M0 pipeline (Vail) -> M1 terrain viewer -> M2 riding physics -> M3 trees and rock -> M4 lifts and landmarks -> M5 snow and atmosphere -> M6 second resort, timed runs, multiplayer.
